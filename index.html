Chinese Long March 7A Explodes After Liftoff
A Chinese Long March 7A rocket exploded approximately 85 seconds after liftoff on Aug 10 2026 from the Wenchang Space Launch Site in Hainan, destroying the ChinaSat-4B communications satellite intended for geostationary orbit. China state news agency Xinhua confirmed the mission was unsuccessful and said the cause was under investigation as of Aug 11 2026. The failure was China's fourth of 2026 in 56 orbital launch attempts and has placed the YF-100 engine, shared across multiple Chinese rocket variants, under mandatory review, raising concern over near-term launch cadence including the Chang'e-7 lunar mission.

Mecca Pact — Saudi, Turkey, Pakistan Sign NATO-Like Deal
Saudi Arabia, Turkey, and Pakistan signed the Mecca Joint Defence Agreement on Aug 7 2026 in Mecca, stipulating that an armed attack against any one of the three states shall be regarded as an attack against all. The pact has a collective-defence provision but analysts and Pakistani officials cautioned against equating it with NATO, noting it is not directed against any specific country.
