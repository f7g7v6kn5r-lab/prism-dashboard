Navy Awards Raytheon $22.9B Tomahawk Deal
The US Navy awarded Raytheon a $22.9 billion, seven-year contract to accelerate Tomahawk cruise missile production to more than 1,000 missiles per year, announced by Acting Secretary of the Navy Hung Cao on Monday August 18, per Reuters and Military Times. The contract formalizes a framework agreement signed with the Pentagon in February 2026. A July report by the Project On Government Oversight estimated current production at approximately 60 missiles per year and warned that manufacturing capacity rather than funding is the binding constraint, with a 47-month delivery lead time once a contract is signed, introducing execution risk to the program regardless of funding levels.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
This contract is the largest single munitions award in recent memory and locks RTX into a decade-long revenue stream, but the solid rocket motor bottleneck signals that the entire Western precision-strike restocking cycle may move slower than procurement headlines suggest.
