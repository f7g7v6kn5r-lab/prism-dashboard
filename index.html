URGENT North America CRITICAL · 85
Roman Space Telescope launches on $4.3B mission
NASA and SpaceX successfully launched the Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope on a Falcon Heavy rocket from Florida on Sunday, opening a $4.3 billion mission to survey dark matter, dark energy, and tens of thousands of exoplanets with 300-megapixel wide-field imaging. The observatory, built around a Hubble-class mirror originally manufactured for a US intelligence satellite, will operate at the Sun-Earth L2 point and is regarded as NASAs next flagship great observatory following the James Webb Space Telescope.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
The Roman launch confirms NASAs ability to execute flagship-class science missions on schedule, reinforcing long-cycle procurement confidence for prime contractors and suppliers across the observatory segment at a time when civil space budgets face political pressure.

The European Space Agency has awarded its first contracts under the European Launcher Challenge, a competition designed to stimulate development of next-generation sovereign launch vehicles and reduce European dependence on non-European providers.
