Landspace Zhuque-3 Booster Recovered but Fire Damages Stage
Chinese commercial launch firm Landspace confirmed it is targeting refurbishment and a reflight of the Zhuque-3 first stage within months of its historic vertical landing on August 19, making it the first private Chinese company to recover an orbital-class booster on land. A post-touchdown fire ignited residual propellant and damaged the landing-gear area, causing the booster to collapse, and the company has begun on-site inspections to assess the path to reuse.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
The landing validates Chinese commercial reusability at the mission level, but booster damage from the post-touchdown fire introduces uncertainty over the reflight timeline and the pace at which Landspace can demonstrate reusability economics comparable to those SpaceX established with Falcon 9.
