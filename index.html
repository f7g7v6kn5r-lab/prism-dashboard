Rocket Lab Neutron fairing completes all pre-flight testing
Rocket Lab announced that the Neutron medium-lift rockets Hungry Hippo fairing section completed all required testing before first flight, including opening and closing of the fairing halves in flight-like conditions. NORMAL Europe HIGH · 68
ESA awards €543.6M across three launcher startups
The European Space Agency signed the first contracts under its European Launcher Challenge, committing 543.6 million euros to Isar Aerospace (197.8 million euros), Rocket Factory Augsburg (186.9 million euros), and PLD Space (158.9 million euros), with a fourth contract with MaiaSpace still being finalised. Funding is released as milestones are met, with Component A covering operational launches through 2030 conditional on reaching orbit by end of 2027, and Component B co-funding vehicle upgrades to be demonstrated by end of 2028.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
ESA structuring 543.6 million euros as milestone-gated payments rather than upfront grants directly ties European public launch investment to demonstrated performance, reducing subsidy risk while forcing three competing startups to reach orbit on a 2027 deadline — a competitive pressure that accelerates Europes sovereign launch capability faster than any single government programme could.

NORMAL North America HIGH · 65
NASA and SpaceX launch $4.3B Roman Space Telescope
NASA and SpaceX successfully launched the Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope aboard a Falcon Heavy from Kennedy Space Center, with the observatory now travelling to the Sun-Earth Lagrange Point 2 on a $4.3 billion mission. Roman will scan broad swaths of the cosmos 1,000 times faster than Hubble, collecting as much data in one month as Hubble could manage in a century, targeting dark matter, dark energy, and potentially habitable exoplanets.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
Roman reaching L2 successfully validates Falcon Heavy as a reliable flagship science delivery vehicle and reinforces NASAs science programme cadence at a moment when budget pressure on non-defence space is intensifying, giving commercial space operators a strong reference mission for heavy-lift payload confidence.

NORMAL Asia-Pacific HIGH · 62
LandSpace lands orbital booster in Chinese commercial first
Chinese commercial launch company LandSpace landed the first stage booster of its ZhuQue-3 rocket at a designated landing zone in Minqin County, Gansu Province, becoming the first commercial Chinese company to recover an orbital-class booster on land. Video shared on social media showed a post-landing fire in the aft section of the vehicle, though LandSpace described the mission as Chinas first successful booster recovery of an orbital-class launch vehicle using landing legs.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
Chinas commercial reusability milestone narrows the technical gap with SpaceX faster than most Western analysts projected, and defence and intelligence operators dependent on assured launch superiority should treat ZhuQue-3s recovery as a structural signal that the Western launch cost advantage will compress within this decade.

Bengaluru-based Kepler Aerospace raised $8 million in a seed round led by Blue Ashva Capital and co-led by Finvolve India Accelerator, the companys first external fundraising since its founding in 2018, reported at around 66.4 crore rupees. URGENT North America CRITICAL · 88
Army awards AeroVironment $464.8M in first directed energy production contract
The US Army awarded AeroVironment a $464.8 million production contract under an Other Transaction Agreement for the Enduring-High Energy Laser programme, marking the first production contract for a directed energy system in US military history. The contract covers delivery of dozens of 30 kW LOCUST laser weapons capable of engaging Group 1 through Group 3 drones, with Vice Chief of Staff General Christopher LaNeve citing the systems ability to defeat unmanned systems as a formation-level capability.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
The first directed energy production award in US military history sets a procurement precedent that will accelerate counter-drone laser budgets across all services, and AeroVironments position as the sole awardee on this landmark contract makes it the benchmark against which every competing laser developer will now be evaluated.
