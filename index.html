Musk cites ToS breach after Starlink restrictions hit Ukraine
SpaceX CEO Elon Musk claimed Ukrainian forces violated Starlink terms of service by using the civilian satellite network for offensive drone strikes, prompting SpaceX to restrict the service; Ukrainian officials disputed the claim. NORMAL Europe MEDIUM · 40
PERSEUS project explores PCSEL lasers for space optical links
Craft Prospect, Fraunhofer Centre for Applied Photonics, and Vector Photonics have launched the PERSEUS project to evaluate Photonic-Crystal Surface-Emitting Laser technology for space-to-space and space-to-ground optical communications, funded through the UK Advanced Connectivity Technologies programme delivered by Innovate UK. The project will test PCSEL compatibility with spacecraft hardware and define the engineering steps toward a commercial solution.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS

India ammunition exports to Europe surge over 1,600 percent
Indian Defence Secretary Rajesh Kumar Singh cited a Bloomberg report showing India exported at least $872 million worth of ammunition and related products to Europe in the four years since Russias invasion of Ukraine, a more than 1,600 percent increase compared with the preceding four-year period. NORMAL Europe HIGH · 68
Helsing unveils sub-$100,000 Contra drone interceptor suite
German company Helsing unveiled the Contra air-defense suite at a product presentation in Cologne, comprising two interceptor missiles — the jet-powered AD-1 Inferno and the solid-rocket AD-2 Ambush — designed to down fast attack drones for under $100,000 per shot, with market readiness expected in the second half of 2027. A credible sub-$100,000 European interceptor designed specifically for Geran-class drones addresses the cost-exchange problem that makes current air defence economically unsustainable, and its 2027 readiness timeline means European procurement offices must evaluate it against Patriot and other systems in current budget cycles.

AI courses of action outpace human planners at Nellis sprint
At Nellis Air Force Base, an AI system built courses of action in under one minute with a 97 percent viability rating, compared with roughly 19 minutes and 48 percent viability for human military planners in the same two-week exercise, War on the Rocks reported. ◆ PRISM ANALYSIS

Britain opts out of Canada-led global defence bank
Britain will not join the Canada-led Defence, Security and Resilience Bank, with the two countries instead agreeing to develop a framework for cooperation between their separate defence-funding initiatives. The two governments were unable to agree on key issues including headquarters location, joint procurement levels, lending to members, and rules around new members, with Canadas bank now focusing more on single sovereign lending while Britains Multilateral Defence Mechanism targets joint procurement financing across NATO allies.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
The split into two distinct institutions rather than one unified global defence bank reduces the collective capital leverage available to NATO allied procurement, potentially leaving smaller alliance members with less favourable financing terms for urgent capability gaps.
