URGENT North America HIGH · 72
SpaceX schedules Starship Flight 15 for Nov. 13
SpaceX is scheduled to launch Starship Flight 15 no earlier than 13 November at 5:45 p.m. EST from its Starbase site in South Texas, with a 75-minute launch window extending to 7:00 p.m. EST. The mission will attempt back-to-back recovery of both the Super Heavy booster and the Ship upper stage at the launch pad, which SpaceX describes as the first full recovery of an orbital rocket in history. The rocket is intended for use on Starlink satellite launches, NASA Artemis lunar missions, and future crewed Mars flights.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
A successful dual recovery would validate the full reusability architecture that underpins SpaceXs cost projections for Starlink replenishment and government launch contracts, compressing the gap between SpaceX and any rival attempting to compete on price or cadence.

NORMAL Europe HIGH · 68
Poland contracts SATIM for SAR imagery analysis
Polish AI firm SATIM has secured a contract from Polands Ministry of National Defence to supply automated analysis software for synthetic aperture radar imagery, supporting the MikroSAR earth-observation programme advanced by a consortium of Finlands ICEYE and Wojskowe Zaklady Lacznosci No 1, a subsidiary of Polish state-run defence group PGZ. SATIM said its solution reaches 90 to 95 percent accuracy for most object classes and can identify objects across approximately 300 classes including vessels, aircraft and vehicles. The company is simultaneously supporting Germanys Rheinmetall on the SPOCK-1 satellite reconnaissance programme, with that automated analysis pipeline now in operational testing phase.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
Automated SAR analysis is becoming a sovereign capability requirement across NATOs eastern flank, creating a durable software services market adjacent to the hardware procurement wave that benefits specialist AI firms with existing government relationships across multiple allied nations.

NORMAL Europe HIGH · 55
Gama raises 15M euros for satellite solar arrays
Paris-based spacetech Gama closed a 15 million euro seed round backed by Transition, Planet A, Draper Associates and Manta Ray to fund the first production line for Wing, its deployable solar array, at its Ivry-sur-Seine site near Paris, and to hire around 60 people. The company, founded in 2022, pivoted from solar sail development to commercial solar arrays after finding solar sail funding was structurally difficult to secure, with the Gama Alpha mission launched in 2023 providing technical foundation for the Wing product. Eclipse Space is the only named customer to date, with a deal announced in September under which Eclipse will build the satellite bus and Gama will build the large solar arrays.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
Gamas pivot from solar sails to production solar arrays reflects a broader European pattern of deep-tech startups repositioning toward near-term commercial satellite infrastructure demand, and the €15 million seed scale signals early-stage investor appetite for in-space power components ahead of constellation build-outs.

NORMAL South Asia MEDIUM · 45
ISRO pensioners seminar surfaces sector anxiety
The ISRO Pensioners Association, representing some 4,000 members, held a seminar titled ISROs Future - Concerns and Anxieties in Thiruvananthapuram, drawing former chairman Dr G Madhavan Nair and retired engineers to debate the direction of Indias space sector reforms. The event was prompted in part by public statements from Pawan Goenka, who heads IN-SPACe, that eventually ISRO would stop manufacturing launch vehicles including PSLVs, with all such work moving to the private sector or public sector units. ◆ PRISM ANALYSIS

NORMAL Europe CRITICAL · 80
Belgium orders NASAMS for Blue Dome air defence
Belgium has contracted for 10 Kongsberg NASAMS short-to-medium-range air defence systems, 20 Rheinmetall Skyranger 30 short-range systems, and an undisclosed number of Thales GM200 MM/C radars to form its layered Blue Dome air defence architecture, with the NASAMS order signed in September and valued at NOK10 billion. Belgium is the 18th country to select NASAMS, with the GM200 radars providing detection, NASAMS handling the missile intercept layer, and Skyranger 30 covering very short-range threats including uncrewed aerial vehicles. The three systems are described as providing complementary capabilities within a layered air defence architecture.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
The 18th national NASAMS adoption cements Kongsbergs and Raytheons system as the default medium-range intercept layer in European air defence architecture, generating recurring integration, software, and munitions demand that extends well beyond the initial contract value.

House blocks Hegseth impeachment in 211-204 vote
The House voted 211 to 204 to send impeachment articles against Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth back to committee, blocking an immediate removal vote, with Republicans largely standing behind the secretary after President Donald Trump signalled strong support. The resolution was filed by Kentucky Republican Rep. The near-certainty of an impeachment vote in January introduces a prolonged period of Pentagon leadership uncertainty that could slow procurement decisions, delay programme approvals, and create an opening for adversaries to probe US resolve at a moment when the Iran conflict and Ukraine negotiations both demand sustained institutional attention.

NORMAL Saudi Arabia HIGH · 72
Saudi crude output rises to 10.563M bpd in October
An OPEC report issued Wednesday shows Saudi Arabia pumped 10.563 million barrels per day in October, up by over two million barrels per day from September and the highest level since the United States and Israel launched the war on Iran in late February, when Saudi Arabia produced 10.882 million barrels per day. OPEC+ combined output reached 41.23 million barrels per day in October, up by about 2.04 million barrels per day from September, aided by rising Russian production after US President Donald Trump lifted sanctions on Russian energy and announced an energy ceasefire between Ukraine and Russia in mid-October. OPEC left its forecasts for global oil demand growth unchanged and made no changes to its 2027 outlook.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
Recovering Gulf output reduces the energy-price shock that has driven US public opposition to the Iran war, potentially easing political pressure on the administration but also reducing the economic leverage underpinning ceasefire diplomacy.

URGENT Romania HIGH · 72
Turla linked to Romanian NATO email server hack
Slovak cybersecurity firm ESET revealed that the Russia-affiliated hacking group Turla, assessed to be linked to Russias FSB, compromised Romanian government Microsoft Exchange servers in December 2025 using a tool suite dubbed Cereal to extract emails referencing NATO and EU institutional addresses. The CerealBox module selected messages with Word or PDF attachments, then compressed and encrypted copies for retrieval by the attackers, with the hack made public on 10 November. ESET had first detected the same malicious code on the server of a Kazakh government agency in November 2025, describing the toolset as a previously undocumented collection of modular malware designed to run on Microsoft Exchange servers.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
Turlas demonstrated ability to silently penetrate NATO-adjacent government email infrastructure for months before detection underlines the persistent cyber intelligence threat to allied communications and the need for real-time network monitoring investment.
