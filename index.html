Beneath todays constellation of developments runs a single structural shift — the US military-commercial boundary is dissolving, with SpaceX winning Pentagon missile defence contracts, Golden Dome expanding commercial participation, and the Army opening test ranges to private industry, signalling that vertically integrated commercial primes are now competing directly with legacy defence contractors for sovereign security roles. Ceasefire talks remain at an impasse.

BREAKING Red Sea, Yemen CRITICAL · 85
Houthi Attacks Resume as Iran Talks Stall
Iran-backed Houthi rebels killed six people aboard the Egyptian-owned cargo ship Tihamah in the Bab el-Mandeb strait on Aug 11, per CNBC and Epoch Times (Aug 12). The brief stated fatalities marked the first deaths on shipping by Houthis since the Iran war began; multiple sources describe these as the first reported fatalities from Red Sea shipping attacks in more than a year, which is a narrower and more accurate framing than since the Iran war began. Four of the six confirmed dead were identified as three Pakistanis and one Indonesian, per Pakistani and Yemeni officials (Epoch Times, Aug 12). On ceasefire status: a senior Iranian source told Reuters on Aug 12 that there are no discussions underway with the US about extending a ceasefire under an interim deal, and that the US must return to the terms of the memorandum of understanding and set a timeline -- the brief claim that Iran insists the US must return to an interim deal is accurate, but the framing that Iran signalled no ceasefire extension is under discussion is confirmed only for the Iranian side.
