Chinese Long March 7A Explodes After Liftoff
A Long March 7A rocket exploded approximately 85 seconds after liftoff on Aug 10 2026 from Wenchang, destroying the ChinaSat-4B communications satellite, according to Chinese state media and livestream footage confirmed by SpaceNews and Reuters.
