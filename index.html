URGENT North America CRITICAL · 85
NASA Roman Space Telescope launches on $4.3B mission
NASA and SpaceX successfully launched the Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope aboard a Falcon Heavy rocket from Florida on Sunday, with SpaceX flight controllers confirming nominal orbit insertion and payload deployment following liftoff. The $4.3 billion observatory, built around a Hubble-class mirror originally constructed for a spy satellite, is expected to discover tens of thousands of exoplanets while investigating the nature of dark matter and dark energy.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
Confirmed orbit insertion establishes SpaceXs Falcon Heavy as a reliable workhorse for high-value sovereign science payloads, directly strengthening the commercial launch market and reducing NASAs dependence on dedicated government launch infrastructure.

URGENT North America CRITICAL · 85
Boeing F-15 ceiling, agreements worth up to $131 billion through 2037
The US Air Force has awarded Boeing an indefinite-delivery, indefinite-quantity contract with a $131.23 billion ceiling for F-15 production, modernisation and sustainment, covering the full F-15 fleet through an ordering period extending to 2036 with work through 2037, according to Defense News and Breaking Defense. The ceiling represents maximum ordering capacity rather than committed expenditure, with only $343,740 obligated at award, but the vehicle consolidates virtually every aspect of the F-15 programme — from new aircraft to upgrades, depot maintenance and foreign military sales — under a single contract.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
A $131.23 billion ordering ceiling for a legacy fighter gives Boeing a long-duration procurement vehicle for its defence business at a time when its commercial aviation operations remain under pressure, though the IDIQ structure means revenue accrues only as individual task orders are placed and executed.
