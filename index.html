BREAKING Yemen CRITICAL · 85
Pentagon: US Strikes Killed 153 Yemen Civilians in 2025
A Pentagon review, obtained by the Associated Press and reported Aug 12, 2026, found that US airstrikes targeting Houthi rebels in Yemen in 2025 killed 153 civilians and injured another 243. The unclassified assessment was sent to Congress as part of an annual civilian casualties report. The review focused on three April 2025 airstrikes during Operation Rough Rider, which the Pentagon determined more likely than not resulted in civilian harm. The report is not yet formally submitted; a Pentagon official said the department will submit it in the coming months. The characterisation in the original brief that this is a newly declassified review is accurate, but the figure of 153 killed is confirmed alongside 243 injured -- the injured figure was absent from the original claim (AP, Aug 12, 2026).
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
Official confirmation of large-scale civilian casualties from US Yemen strikes raises political and legal risk for defence contractors supplying munitions and targeting systems used in that campaign, and could affect future authorisations and contract renewals.
