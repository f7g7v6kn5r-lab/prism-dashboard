BREAKING Asia-Pacific CRITICAL · 88
China lands Zhuque-3 booster — first for commercial sector
LandSpace successfully recovered the first stage of its Zhuque-3 Y2 rocket at a downrange pad in Minqin County, Gansu Province, on Aug. 18, 2026, marking the first time a Chinese private company has landed an orbital-class booster using landing legs -- confirmed by SpaceNews, NASASpaceFlight, and Space.com. However, per Particle News (Aug. 19, 2026), residual propellant ignited shortly after touchdown, causing a post-landing fire that damaged the landing-gear area and caused the booster to collapse; LandSpace has begun on-site inspections. The milestone is also not the first booster recovery by a Chinese entity overall: state-owned CASC previously recovered the Chang Zheng 10B at sea. The claim that China is closing the reusability gap with SpaceX at accelerating speed is accurate in direction, though no reuse of a recovered Chinese commercial booster has yet been completed.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
A reusable commercial Chinese orbital booster compresses the cost-per-kilogram curve for Chinese constellation operators and directly threatens Western launch pricing assumptions underpinning RKLB and other smallsat launch providers.
