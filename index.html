China lands orbital booster in global first
LandSpace completed the second flight of its Zhuque-3 rocket on Aug 19, 2026 (CST) and successfully recovered the first stage on land at Minqin County, Gansu Province, becoming the first Chinese commercial company to land an orbital-class booster on land. Sources as of Aug 19, 2026 note that Zhuque-3 joins Falcon 9, New Glenn, and the state-owned Chang Zheng 10B as one of only four flight-proven orbital-class partially-reusable rockets -- the capability is no longer US-exclusive but it is not exclusively commercial-Chinese-first, as CASC achieved a separate booster recovery at sea in July 2026.
