Putin hails Musk as genius amid Starlink curbs
Russian President Vladimir Putin called Elon Musk a genius while touring an exhibition on the sidelines of the Russia-Africa Summit in Moscow, noting that Starlink operates nearly 10,000 satellites against Russias state-backed Rassvet constellation of just over 30. Obria Nano satellite begins on-orbit tests
Ukrainian startup Himera and Danish manufacturer GomSpace confirmed their Obria Nano satellite, launched October 1 aboard the SpaceX Transporter-18 rideshare mission, is now undergoing on-orbit tests, with the companies planning a constellation to provide communications coverage across Ukraine. The project is one of at least two European-backed initiatives to give Ukraine greater control over satellite communications after disruptions to Starlink and Starshield services in mid-October affected Ukrainian military drone operations.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS

URGENT Middle East CRITICAL · 95
Iran war costs US at least $47.1bn so far
The Pentagon disclosed to Congress that the US-Iran war has cost at least $47.1 billion as of October 16, with more than $99 billion of that needed to replace expended munitions, while the US Air Force spent $7.6 billion on fighter and bomber units and the US Army spent $2.1 billion to sustain its troops in the Middle East. The estimate does not include costs to repair military bases damaged in Iranian attacks or fuel costs for ships and aircraft, and the Pentagons inspector general separately warned of strategic inventory shortfalls as the war continues.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
A confirmed $99 billion munitions replacement requirement, combined with active preparations for a post-midterm strike campaign, creates the most urgent and quantified near-term demand signal for munitions producers and replenishment contractors in the current conflict cycle.

Hegseth fires Joint Staff planner over leak claims
Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth fired Army Lt Gen Brett Sylvia, director of strategy, plans and policy for the Joint Staff, with a senior Pentagon official accusing Sylvia of failing three polygraph examinations related to leaking classified information, and the matter referred to the Justice Department for potential further legal action. URGENT Middle East CRITICAL · 88
IRGC says three tankers hit mines in Hormuz
Irans Revolutionary Guards stated that three oil tankers caught fire after striking mines while attempting to cross the Strait of Hormuz via what the IRGC described as an unauthorised southern route, saying the vessels had switched off all radar and navigation systems and disregarded warnings from the IRGC navy. The Strait has remained largely closed since the war began on February 28, a waterway that normally carries about one-fifth of global oil and liquefied natural gas supplies, and the IRGC separately claimed its electronic warfare unit captured a US MQM-170 drone in the western Strait of Hormuz.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
IRGC claims of additional Hormuz mining incidents, if independently confirmed, would further compress the already restricted maritime corridor for global energy supply and intensify pressure on Gulf state infrastructure and insurance markets simultaneously with the pre-midterm diplomatic window closing.

Hegseth-Congress rift threatens $1.5 trillion defence plan
Republican lawmakers are openly criticising Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth for his confrontational approach to Congress, with senior House Armed Services Committee member Rep Austin Scott and Republican Senator Lisa Murkowski describing his conduct as shockingly unprofessional, according to the Washington Post which interviewed 30 people involved in congressional national security matters. URGENT Middle East CRITICAL · 80
Iran secret network imported $2bn past blockade
A Reuters investigation found that Iran built a secret network of more than 5,000 military agencies sharing the same Tehran address as the Ministry of Defense to import at least $2 billion in hardware, explosives and heavy-duty trucks since January 2025, evading the US naval blockade and Western sanctions. A single agency, MOD 346428, imported rocket propellants, explosives, weapons components critical for drones and missiles, and heavy-duty trucks that can serve as missile launchers, with hardware recorded as originating from China, Russia and Germany.

URGENT North America CRITICAL · 78
USS Spruance reaches 342 days deployed
The Arleigh Burke-class destroyer USS Spruance reached 342 days deployed as of October 29, far exceeding the Navys commonly cited seven-month deployment benchmark, having departed Naval Base San Diego on November 21, 2025, as part of the USS Abraham Lincoln carrier strike group and remaining at sea after the carrier group moved on. Pentagon treats base freezer failures as suspected hack

Army tests adaptive camouflage coatings in Arctic
Army Corps of Engineers and Army Research Laboratory researchers tested novel camouflage coatings in an experiment at Pituffik, Greenland, as part of the International Cooperative Engagement Program for Polar Research and the Office of Naval Research, with researchers inspired by Arctic foxes that change color from white in winter to brown in summer. Canada seeks 120 UAS in C$32M procurement
The Canadian Department of National Defence has an open request for proposals for the procurement of domestic general-purpose uncrewed aerial systems to equip Canadian Army units supporting NATO commitments in Latvia and forces operating in the Arctic, with submissions due November 3, 2026.
