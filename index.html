Rocket Labs Neutron Hungry Hippo clears all tests
Rocket Lab announced on 27 August that the Hungry Hippo fairing section of its Neutron medium-lift vehicle has completed all required testing, including opening and closing the permanently attached fairings in flight-like conditions. The company has also completed over 400 Archimedes engine hot-fires and signed multiple contracts including one with the US Space Forces Space Systems Command, with Neutron targeting a debut launch before the end of 2026.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
Neutron clearing hardware milestones on schedule narrows the window before Rocket Lab competes directly for medium-lift national security payloads, pressuring ULA and increasing launch optionality for Space Systems Command.

NORMAL East Asia HIGH · 72
LandSpace lands orbital booster in Chinese first
Chinese commercial launch company LandSpace successfully recovered the first stage of its ZhuQue-3 rocket on 19 August, marking the first-ever land recovery of an orbital-class booster by a commercial Chinese company, touching down in Minqin County, Gansu Province roughly eight minutes after liftoff. A post-landing fire broke out in the aft section of the vehicle, though LandSpace confirmed the recovery as successful and described the flight as pivotal in transitioning ZhuQue-3 from technology demonstration toward engineering application of reusability.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
Chinas commercial reusable launch sector has crossed a structural threshold that closes the gap with SpaceX, accelerating competitive pressure on Western launch providers competing for both national and commercial payloads across Asian and emerging markets.

NORMAL Europe HIGH · 68
ESA commits €543.6M to three launch startups
ESA signed the first contracts under its European Launcher Challenge, awarding €197.8 million to Isar Aerospace, €186.9 million to Rocket Factory Augsburg and €158.9 million to PLD Space, with a fourth contract involving ArianeGroup subsidiary MaiaSpace still being finalised. Each contract splits into two components — operational launch services through 2030 and vehicle upgrade demonstrations through 2028 — with funding released only as milestones are met, and orbit must be achieved by end of 2027 to unlock the first component.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
The milestone-gated structure means capital flows only on performance, reducing ESA risk while creating a competitive multi-supplier European launch market that reduces dependence on any single vehicle and opens procurement competition for allied governments.

SpaceX unveils 10-pad Starbase Louisiana spaceport
SpaceX announced plans for Starbase Louisiana at Pecan Island in Vermilion Parish, describing it as the largest private spaceport in the world, with 10 Starship launch pads, a large production facility and a first launch targeted for 2029. NORMAL North America HIGH · 62
NASA and SpaceX launch $4.3B Roman telescope
A Falcon Heavy launched the Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope from Launch Complex 39A at Kennedy Space Center, placing it on course for the Sun-Earth Lagrange Point 2 on a mission valued at $4.3 billion. Roman carries a 300-megapixel wide-field camera and will scan the cosmos approximately 1,000 times faster than Hubble, targeting dark energy, exoplanets and large-scale cosmic structure over a five-year primary mission.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
Roman establishes a new data-volume benchmark for space-based science that will drive demand for ground-segment processing infrastructure and create downstream commercial opportunities in data analytics and AI-enabled astronomy.

URGENT North America CRITICAL · 90
US Army awards AeroVironment $464.8M laser contract
The US Army awarded AeroVironment a $464.8 million production contract under an Other Transaction Agreement for the Enduring-High Energy Laser programme, which Army Vice Chief of Staff General Christopher LaNeve described as the first production contract for a directed energy system in US military history. AeroVironment will deliver dozens of 30 kW LOCUST laser weapons designed to defeat Group 1 through Group 3 unmanned aerial systems, with the award coming after soldiers reported disappointing performance from the earlier DE M-SHORAD prototypes in the Middle East.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
The first directed energy production award in US military history establishes a commercial baseline for laser-kill economics against drone threats, directly benefiting AeroVironment and signalling that counter-UAS procurement is transitioning from prototype to programme-of-record scale.

Army awards $192M to Palantir and Anduril for TITAN
The US Army moved its Tactical Intelligence Targeting Access Node programme from prototyping to production with a combined $192 million award, allocating $127 million to prime contractor Palantir Technologies and $65 million to Anduril Industries. Army tests AI CUAS turret at $7 per drone kill
The Pentagon and its agencies are testing the Edda portable autonomous AI-driven hard-kill counter-UAS turret, developed by Spotter Global and 9 Mothers, against coordinated drone swarms including radio-silent first-person-view UAS threats. NORMAL United States HIGH · 55
Marine Corps seeks CT scanners for body armor cracks
The US Marine Corps issued a Request for Information due 2 October for commercial automated computed tomography scanners capable of detecting cracks and delaminations in Enhanced Small Arms Protective Inserts and Lightweight Plates, with ten units planned across the continental US, Japan, Hawaii and Guam. The Enhanced Armor Inspection System would replace current manual tap-and-X-ray methods, applying the same CT technology used in medical imaging and airport bomb detection to create detailed 3D images of ceramic and polyethylene armor layers.
