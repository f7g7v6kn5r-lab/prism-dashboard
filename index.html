URGENT North America CRITICAL · 95
SpaceX Starship Flight 14 targets first orbital mission
SpaceX opened a 75-minute launch window at 7:15 a.m. Central Time on 28 September for Flight 14 of its Starship-Super Heavy rocket, the first planned to send Starship into orbit around Earth. The mission will attempt to deploy 26 Starlink V3 satellites, which SpaceX says deliver 1 Tbps of downlink and 160 Gbps of uplink capacity, a 10x downlink and 22x uplink improvement over Starlink V2. The flight is expected to reach approximately 275 km altitude, complete approximately six orbits over nearly 10 hours, and conclude with splashdown in the Pacific Ocean west of Chile.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
A successful orbital insertion and V3 satellite deployment would validate Starships role as a commercial heavy-lift platform and materially accelerate the Starlink constellation capacity curve, raising competitive pressure on every rival broadband satellite operator and altering the economics of future LEO connectivity contracts.

NORMAL North America HIGH · 60
NASA moves PRIMA telescope into Phase B development
NASA announced that the Probe far-Infrared Mission for Astrophysics, PRIMA, has entered Phase B development, with a maximum project cost of $1.2 billion excluding launch, and will be developed by JPL in collaboration with Goddard Space Flight Center, Marshall Space Flight Center, and the space agencies of Canada, France, Germany, Japan, South Korea, and the United Kingdom. PRIMA introduces a new Probe Explorers class within NASAs Explorers Program and is scheduled to launch in 2033. The California Institute of Technologys Infrared Processing and Analysis Center is designated as the mission science center where raw data from the telescope will be received, processed, and archived.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
A $1.2 billion multi-nation science mission entering Phase B creates a near-term procurement pipeline for infrared detector suppliers, cryogenic systems integrators, and international partner agencies, with the Caltech IPAC science centre designation adding a US academic anchor to a broadly distributed industrial base.

SpaceX Crew-13 crew arrives at Kennedy for Oct. 1 launch
The four-member SpaceX Crew-13 crew arrived at Kennedy Space Center on Saturday, targeting a launch no earlier than 11:10 a.m. EDT on 1 October aboard the Dragon spacecraft Grace. NORMAL North America HIGH · 52
Moog expands Niagara Falls clean room by over 80 percent
Moog Inc. completed and commissioned a new ISO Class 8 clean room at its Niagara Falls facility, increasing dedicated clean room capacity by more than 80 percent and enabling continuous multi-shift operations for thruster assembly, integration, and hot-fire testing. Senior Vice President Mark Covelli cited missile defence, space-based interceptor, and Golden Dome programs as the primary demand drivers for the expanded manufacturing footprint. NORMAL North America MEDIUM · 45
Ethereal Space wins $27.4M NOAA SBEM task order
Ethereal Space announced it was awarded Task Order 1 under NOAAs ProTech 2.0 Space-Based Environmental Monitoring program, with a total contract value of approximately $27.4 million and initial data deliveries scheduled to begin 1 December 2026. The 24-month task order covers GNSS radio occultation, thermospheric neutral-density measurements, and multispectral visible and infrared imagery from Ethereals commercial small satellite constellation. CEO and Co-Founder Shawn Cochran said the award represents an important transition from building and demonstrating new observing capabilities to delivering operational environmental data at scale.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
A NOAA operational data award to a small commercial constellation operator signals continued US government willingness to procure environmental intelligence from emerging commercial providers, creating a template for follow-on task orders under the broader ProTech 2.0 framework that benefits the wider commercial Earth observation sub-sector.

URGENT Europe CRITICAL · 93
Russia plans $202.6B defence budget for 2027
Russian government budget documents seen by Reuters show plans to spend 17.1 trillion roubles, or $202.6 billion, on defence in 2027, a 27 percent increase over the 13.5 trillion roubles, or $159.8 billion, originally budgeted and the highest figure since the start of the war in Ukraine in 2022. Total defence spending across the next three years is projected at 50 trillion roubles, or $591.7 billion, with the government simultaneously raising its 2026 budget deficit estimate to 3.2 percent of GDP from 1.6 percent previously. A new windfall tax on metals and mining companies was expected to raise about 200 billion roubles, or $2.4 billion, annually to help finance the growing deficit.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
A 27 percent real-terms uplift in Russian military spending, sustained across a three-year envelope of $591.7 billion, structurally validates NATO member rearmament timelines and intensifies the demand signal for air defence, long-range precision strike, and counter-UAS systems across the European industrial base.

URGENT Europe CRITICAL · 82
EU approves five joint defence projects under EDIP
EU member states approved five projects for joint defence-industrial investment covering drones and counter-drone systems, air and missile defence, maritime and seabed defence, space, and protection of Europes eastern flank, making them eligible for funding via the 1.5 billion euro European Defence Industry Program. The European Commission has allocated 325 million euros for European Defence Projects of Common Interest within EDIP, with Commissioner for Defence Andrius Kubilius calling the approval a major breakthrough that will close critical capability gaps, with an envisaged funding ambition of around 190 billion euros by 2036. On average, 18 EU countries participate in each project, with Ukraine part of four of the five projects.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
Five approved joint investment categories with 325 million euros allocated create near-term procurement entry points for European prime contractors and sub-tier suppliers in drone, counter-drone, missile defence, and seabed domains at a moment when Russian spending escalation is hardening political will for rapid disbursement.

URGENT Asia-Pacific CRITICAL · 80
US Army to store Typhon and HIMARS at Kadena in October
Japans Defense Ministry announced the US Army will temporarily store its Typhon mid-range missile system and HIMARS at Kadena Air Base on Okinawa starting in mid-October, moving the systems from the Japan Maritime Self-Defense Forces Kanoya Air Base on Kyushu via US Air Force Globemaster III aircraft. NORMAL North America HIGH · 68
Court upholds Pentagon blacklisting of Anthropic
A US Court of Appeals in Washington ruled 2-1 to uphold the Pentagons designation of Anthropic as a national security supply chain risk, barring the San Francisco-based AI company from military contracts after it refused to allow its products to be used for autonomous weapons or mass surveillance. The majority opinion by Judge Gregory Katsas sided with Defense Secretary Pete Hegseths argument that Anthropics safety restrictions could cause AI models to shut down unexpectedly and cause important military operations to fail. Anthropic said it respectfully disagrees with the decision and is considering its options, including review by the full appeals court.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
The ruling establishes a binding judicial precedent that the Pentagon may exclude AI suppliers whose safety guardrails conflict with autonomous weapons requirements, creating a competitive moat for AI firms willing to accept military-use terms and raising the barrier to entry for safety-first AI developers seeking defence contracts.

UK MoD gains access to Ukraines battlefield AI platform

NORMAL France / NATO HIGH · 72
Thales in advanced talks on HexaForce C2 with NATO states
French defence firm Thales said it is in quite advanced discussions with a number of NATO countries on deploying its AI-powered command-and-control system HexaForce, following tests during NATOs Coalition Warrior Interoperability Exercise in Poland in June. NORMAL United States HIGH · 68
US Navy opens hub to field autonomous combat systems
The US Navy established the Robot and Autonomous Systems Warfighting Development Center at Joint Expeditionary Base Little Creek, Virginia, to train sailors on autonomous technology and develop warfighting concepts and command frameworks before robotic systems deploy fleet-wide. The center will test emerging systems in environments that mimic those in which they will be deployed, including at sea, under stress, against simulated threats, and during degraded communication environments. Chief of Naval Operations Admiral Daryl Caudle said a prototype is not combat power and defined the intended cycle as field, employ, learn, improve, and scale.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
A dedicated Navy centre focused on human-machine teaming and autonomous system integration accelerates the transition from experimental robotics to combat-ready fleet capability, signalling near-term procurement demand for proven autonomous systems across surface and undersea domains.

NORMAL South Korea HIGH · 60
Satrec Initiative to build four optical sats for Hanwha
South Korean firm Satrec Initiative is manufacturing four satellites to gather 10-centimeter-class optical imagery to complement parent company Hanwha Aerospaces 64-satellite synthetic-aperture-radar constellation, which is designed to provide 30-minute revisit by 2031. Satrec Initiative Executive Vice President Eugene Kim said the combined constellation is expected to open up new opportunities to provide Satellite as a Service in the defence and intelligence field, with customers also having access to imagery analysis from Satrec Initiative spinoff SI Analytics. Hanwha satellites will operate in very low Earth orbit at altitudes of less than 400 kilometers to achieve the target resolution, beginning with a test satellite near 500 kilometers before lowering the orbit incrementally.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
Integrating AI-enabled analysis directly into a dual-mode SAR and optical constellation positions Hanwha to compete at the highest tier of government intelligence tasking, raising the competitive bar for incumbent EO providers in the defence ISR market.
