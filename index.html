Rocket Lab Neutron fairing clears all tests before first flight
Rocket Labs Hungry Hippo fairing system, permanently attached to the Neutron first stage and designed to open in flight to deploy the second stage, has completed all required testing including open-and-close cycles in flight-like conditions, the company announced on August 27. NORMAL North America HIGH · 60
Stoke Space raises $1B, targets early 2027 for first launch
Stoke Space has raised $1 billion via its Series E capital campaign, bringing total investor haul to $2.3 billion, and is targeting early 2027 for the first-ever launch of its fully reusable Nova Pathfinder rocket, which the company is positioning as a successor to SpaceXs Falcon 9 as that vehicle moves toward retirement. Brent crude tops $100 as Iran-US strikes escalate in Gulf
Brent crude rose to $100.19 a barrel on Wednesday, its highest since July 24, after US forces destroyed five Iranian crude oil tankers overnight and Iran retaliated with missile attacks on US forces in Jordan and attacks on shipping, with US Secretary of State Marco Rubio stating Washington would keep striking Iranian tankers in response to attempted attacks on US warships. Wall Street indices recorded small losses, European stocks fell to one-week lows, and a senior analyst at Swissquote told Reuters that optimism for a peace agreement was fading as September began.

A declared and repeated US policy of tanker destruction combined with Iranian counter-strikes on US forces in Jordan marks a structural escalation threshold that removes the scenario of a quick de-escalation and forces defence investors to price in an extended high-optempo posture with accelerating munitions consumption.

US Central Command destroyed five Iranian crude oil carriers — M/T Kaviz, M/T Charminar, M/T Horizon 1, M/T Riesco in the Gulf of Oman, and M/T Derya near Kharg Island — after Irans Revolutionary Guard launched ballistic missiles at a US Navy warship twice over two days, with CENTCOM stating the warship evaded both attacks and no American personnel were harmed. URGENT Middle East CRITICAL · 97
Houthis strike Saudi Aramco sites, 73 injured
The Houthi movement launched dozens of ballistic missiles and drones targeting Saudi Aramco facilities in Abha, Najran, Jizan and Khamis Mushait Air Base, setting the Aramco refinery at Jazan on the Red Sea ablaze and wounding at least 73 civilians, with Houthi spokesman Yahya Saree claiming the strikes were in response to 121 Saudi air strikes over the preceding three days. Saudi officials confirmed the attacks and said they would retaliate, while global oil prices surged toward the $100 per barrel mark.

Successful strikes on Aramco infrastructure on the Red Sea — Saudis primary remaining energy export corridor given Houthi blockade of the Bab al-Mandeb Strait — directly threaten the marginal barrel keeping global energy markets supplied and make every further escalation in Yemen a systemic market event rather than a regional one.

Pentagon polygraphs ~50 Joint Staff officials over munitions leak
The Pentagon subjected roughly 50 members of the Joint Staff to polygraph examinations in August as part of an investigation into leaks of classified information about US weapons stockpiles, with investigators specifically asking whether personnel had disclosed information about diminished US munitions stocks including long-range missiles and Patriot air-defence interceptors. NORMAL Saudi Arabia / Turkey / Pakistan HIGH · 72
Mecca pact tests Indias regional strategy
The Mecca Joint Defense Agreement signed by Pakistan, Saudi Arabia and Turkey on August 7 has established a secretariat in Saudi Arabia led by a Pakistani secretary-general, with a meeting in Istanbul on August 31 initiating work on an accession roadmap and talks covering counterterrorism, defence production, interoperability, air defence, drones, autonomous systems, electronic warfare and artificial intelligence. The Jerusalem Post analysis notes the pact raises a strategic question for India over whether the collective-defence arrangement will remain mainly political or develop into an operational military structure.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
A formalising Pakistan-Saudi Arabia-Turkey defence bloc with AI, drone and electronic warfare cooperation on its agenda shifts the regional military balance in ways that directly affect Indian and Gulf state threat calculations.

NORMAL United States CRITICAL · 80
Air Force seeks AI drone swarms for cargo plane bases
The US Air Force has issued a Small Business Innovation Research solicitation, with an October 21 deadline, seeking AI-controlled drone swarms launched from cargo aircraft to autonomously defend forward air bases and aircraft from attack, transforming transport planes into what the solicitation calls adaptive force-multiplying launch platforms. The requirement encompasses three missions: high-altitude ISR motherships, self-protection for mobility aircraft in contested airspace, and autonomous perimeter defence of forward bases, with the solicitation noting that Iranian strikes destroyed or damaged American tankers and AWACS aircraft at Persian Gulf airbases during the Iran war.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
An Air Force SBIR for AI-autonomous base-defence swarms deployable from existing cargo aircraft opens a significant new contract pathway for small autonomy and drone firms and signals that the Iran war has directly accelerated requirements for contested-airbase survivability.

Northrop Grumman develops AI-enhanced smaller IBCS
Northrop Grumman is developing an AI-improved version of its Integrated Battle Command System called IBCS Adaptive Framework, a smaller and more mobile variant of the system already in service with Polish forces and being introduced to US forces, amid reported interest in IBCS from 20 countries. NORMAL South Korea CRITICAL · 80
South Korea sets record $53.9B defence budget
South Korea announced a defence budget of KRW 73.28 trillion, approximately $53.9 billion, for the coming year, an 8.2 percent increase driven by deepening security concerns about North Korea, China and Russia, and a desire for greater self-reliance as US President Donald Trump scales back planned military exercises with Seoul. The Ministry of National Defense outlined eight major spending priorities, including a 35 percent increase in funding for the transfer of wartime operational control from the United States, with the goal of completing the OPCON transition by 2029.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
A record South Korean defence budget with a strong self-reliance mandate accelerates domestic procurement and signals sustained demand for Korean-made platforms and technology in a market that is increasingly an export competitor as well as a customer.

Polish Deputy Prime Minister and National Defence Minister Wladyslaw Kosiniak-Kamysz said at the MSPO defence show in Kielce on September 8 that Poland has been allocated up to 43.7 billion euros ($50.8 billion) in low-interest loans for military acquisitions under the European Unions Security Action For Europe scheme.
