Rocket Labs Neutron fairing completes all testing
Rocket Lab announced on 27 August that Neutrons Hungry Hippo fairing section completed its full test campaign, including opening and closing of the fairing halves in flight-like conditions, and has received all required subsystems including thermal protection. NORMAL North America HIGH · 62
NASA Roman telescope launched on Falcon Heavy to L2
NASA and SpaceX launched the Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope on a Falcon Heavy from Kennedy Space Center at 7:26 AM EDT, sending the $4.3 billion observatory toward the Sun-Earth Lagrange Point 2. NORMAL North America CRITICAL · 90
US Army awards AeroVironment $464.8M laser production contract
The US Army awarded AeroVironment a $464.8 million production contract under an Other Transaction Agreement for the Enduring-High Energy Laser programme, marking the first production contract for a directed energy system in US military history. Under the agreement, AeroVironment will deliver dozens of 30 kW LOCUST laser weapons capable of defeating groups one through three unmanned aerial systems, ranging from miniature UAVs to the size of the RQ-7B Shadow.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
The first directed-energy production contract in US military history establishes a programme-of-record precedent that is likely to accelerate competing laser system procurements and structurally disadvantages kinetic interceptor suppliers in the counter-drone segment.

Pentagon tests AI CUAS turret against drone swarms
The Pentagon and its agencies are testing the Edda portable autonomous AI-driven hard-kill counter-UAS turret, supplied by Spotter Global and 9 Mothers, against coordinated drone swarms, with the supplier claiming an engagement cost starting at $7 per target. NORMAL United States HIGH · 60
Marine Corps seeks CT scanners to detect cracked body armour
The US Marine Corps issued a Request for Information due October 2 seeking high-resolution computed tomography scanners to detect cracks and delaminations in individual hard armour plates, including Enhanced Small Arms Protective Inserts and Marine Corps Lightweight Plates. The Enhanced Armor Inspection System calls for 10 CT scanners to be deployed across six continental US sites and locations in Japan, Hawaii and Guam, replacing current manual tap-and-X-ray inspection methods with automated 3D imaging.

NORMAL United Kingdom MEDIUM · 42
AI scribes may miss clinical cues, Edinburgh study finds
A review of 27 studies by University of Edinburgh researchers found that ambient AI scribes, which integrate speech-to-text technology with AI to capture patient notes, can miss facial expressions, gestures and emotional state, and tend to prioritise clinical information at the expense of patients personal illness narratives. The review also found that AI scribe use may lead to deskilling of clinicians and that patients may be hesitant to reveal sensitive information when recordings are active, with 40 percent of UK GPs now reporting use of the tools.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
Findings that AI scribes systematically filter non-verbal and sensitive clinical signals are directly relevant to defence medical programmes deploying ambient AI in austere or battlefield triage settings, where missed behavioural cues can affect casualty assessment accuracy.
