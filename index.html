NORMAL North America HIGH · 55
DARPA awards SpaceNukes OTA for reactor control
Space Nuclear Power Corporation announced that DARPA awarded it an Other Transaction Agreement under the Fast Capacitance program to develop and ground-test advanced autonomous reactor control technologies, building on the 2018 KRUSTY full-power ground test conducted by key members of the same team at Los Alamos National Laboratory. SpaceNukes and Los Alamos National Laboratory will design, build, and conduct a follow-on nuclear ground test of a Kilopower reactor incorporating active control rod operation, an advance beyond the passive architecture demonstrated in KRUSTY. Integration work will be conducted at SpaceNukes workshop in Medina, Ohio, led by Chief Engineer Marc Gibson.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
A DARPA OTA for autonomous nuclear reactor control brings space nuclear power a measurable step closer to operational readiness for national security missions, expanding the addressable market for both power-dense military satellites and long-duration deep-space government payloads.

URGENT North America CRITICAL · 92
Pentagon and Lockheed agree decade F-35 framework
The Pentagon and Lockheed Martin announced a framework agreement covering at least 850 US F-35 aircraft across 10 production lots, initial spares, and 10 years of sustainment and modification kits, with production held at 156 aircraft annually; Lockheed will self-fund at least $7 billion in unspecified F-35 programme initiatives as part of the deal. The agreement is contingent on congressional approval and funding, and no total contract value or per-jet price was disclosed; formal pricing and scheduling will be negotiated once lawmakers act.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
A decade of production certainty at 156 jets per year removes annual renegotiation risk for Lockheed and its supply chain, but the framework carries zero obligated value until Congress appropriates funds, making the near-term budget cycle the decisive variable for investors pricing in the upside.

URGENT North America CRITICAL · 82
US Army seeks new makers for 155mm howitzer barrels
The US Army issued a sources sought notice seeking contractors capable of manufacturing up to 10 howitzer barrels per month within three years, citing concerns that the governments only barrel plant, the aging Watervliet Arsenal in New York, cannot keep pace with increased demand. Companies must respond by November 23 and are asked to detail boring, milling, rifling and chrome bore coating capabilities as well as capacity to deliver an initial quantity of 40 cannon tubes.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
The Armys search for alternative barrel manufacturers exposes a single-point-of-failure in US artillery industrial capacity at a moment of elevated NATO and Indo-Pacific demand, and a credible response by November 23 would signal whether private industry can absorb the overflow before allied commitments outpace Watervliet output.

NORMAL Europe CRITICAL · 80
Rheinmetall opens UK gun barrel factory after 20-year gap
Rheinmetall officially opened its UK Gun Hall in Telford, making barrels and breeches for 120mm and 155mm weapon systems including the RCH 155 howitzer and the Challenger 3 main battle tank programme, the first large-calibre gun barrel production on British soil in 20 years. Defence Secretary Wes Streeting attended the ceremony alongside Rheinmetall AG chief executive Armin Papperger; the facility currently employs 35 people with plans to support more than 100 skilled jobs as production scales.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
Rheinmetalls Telford facility restores a sovereign UK manufacturing node for critical artillery components at the precise moment NATO allies face barrel and ammunition shortfalls, reducing dependence on a single US government arsenal and creating a template for further European re-industrialisation.

NORMAL Europe CRITICAL · 78
Royal Navy ASW frigate shortfall confirmed at five hulls
A UK parliamentary defence committee found the Royal Navy has only five Type 23 frigates for anti-submarine warfare in service against a stated requirement of eight, and analysis suggests that number could remain as low as five for the next three years. The committee warned the shortfall weakens the UKs ability to track Russian submarines in the High North and to protect ballistic missile submarines entering and leaving their base at Faslane, Scotland, and recommended no further towed-sonar Type 23s leave service until replaced one-for-one by Type 26 frigates, of which eight have been ordered from BAE Systems.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
A formally confirmed three-hull ASW deficit, persisting until at least 2030, strengthens the investment case for BAE Systems Type 26 programme acceleration and for adjacent underwater surveillance and uncrewed systems programmes the Royal Navy is actively developing.

NORMAL United States HIGH · 55
Marines sue Pentagon over DMDC data breach
Two Marines filed a class-action lawsuit in federal court alleging the Pentagon ignored known cybersecurity vulnerabilities at the Defense Manpower Data Center for approximately nine months before an unauthorised party accessed unencrypted files containing names, Social Security numbers, birth dates and military occupations. Pentagon awards $41M ketamine wafer contract
The Defense Department awarded a nearly $41 million development contract to Singapore-based iX Biopharma to develop Wafermine, a sublingual ketamine wafer described by the manufacturer as intended for acute moderate to severe battlefield pain.
