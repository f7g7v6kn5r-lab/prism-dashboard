URGENT Europe CRITICAL · 82
Russian satellite closes within 500m of ICEYE radar craft
Russian military satellite Cosmos 2612 manoeuvred from more than 320 kilometres away to approximately 4.8 kilometres from ICEYE-X36, a US-registered commercial radar satellite that supplies imagery to Ukraine, between Thursday and Friday last week according to space-tracking company LeoLabs. Over the weekend it moved between positions ahead of and behind the ICEYE satellite, at times coming within roughly 500 metres, while ICEYE chief executive Rafal Modrzewski confirmed to Reuters that the company moved its own satellite in response, which appeared to prompt further Russian manoeuvres.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
A deliberate close approach to a commercial intelligence satellite supplying a combatant nation establishes a live precedent for orbital coercion that every commercial remote-sensing operator and their government customers must now price into constellation architecture and insurance risk.

SpaceX deploys 32 payloads on Bandwagon-5 rideshare
SpaceX launched its Falcon 9 rocket from Space Launch Complex 40 at Cape Canaveral Space Force Station carrying 32 payloads including the uncrewed Starfall reentry capsule on the Bandwagon-5 rideshare mission, after a Monday scrub due to poor weather in the booster recovery zone. NORMAL North America CRITICAL · 80
Pentagon signs BAE framework for up to 25,000 APKWS kits a year
The Defense Department signed a framework agreement with BAE Systems to buy up to 25,000 Advanced Precision Kill Weapon System guidance kits per year for as long as seven years, with a stated path to increase output to 35,000 kits per year for US allies through Foreign Military Sales once partner nations formally request them. BAE builds the kits in Hudson, New Hampshire and Austin, Texas and reported delivery of its 100,000th kit in February; the framework carries no announced cost figure and still requires congressional approval and funding.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
A seven-year demand signal at the 25,000-unit-per-year ceiling gives BAE Systems a durable production planning horizon for the cheapest proven drone-intercept munition in US inventory, directly benefiting suppliers and sub-contractors across the APKWS guidance kit supply chain at a moment when drone attrition rates in two active conflict zones are accelerating consumption.

NORMAL Asia-Pacific HIGH · 72
Australia launches tender for medium-range ground-based air defence
Australias Department of Defence launched an industry solicitation for Project Land 6504, a medium-range ground-based air defence system for the Australian Army, on October 13, with vendors from Europe, Israel, South Korea, Turkey and the United States already expressing interest. The National Defence Strategy 2026 explicitly prioritised medium-range active missile defence, citing Chinas intercontinental ballistic missile test into the South Pacific in July and a Chinese naval task group that circumnavigated Australia in early 2025 as the primary threat drivers.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
A formal competitive tender open to vendors from five regions for a terminal-phase ballistic missile defence capability represents one of the largest air defence procurement opportunities in the Indo-Pacific this year, and directly benefits suppliers with mature hit-to-kill or active homing intercept pedigrees competing for a first-mover position in Australian sovereign missile defence.
