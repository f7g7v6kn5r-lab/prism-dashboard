Pentagon removes Anthropic from 90% of classified AI work
The Pentagons chief technology officer stated that approximately 90 percent of the Defense Departments classified artificial intelligence workloads previously running on Anthropic technology have been transitioned to different model providers, and all Anthropic technology was removed from the Maven Smart Systems programme months ago. NORMAL Europe HIGH · 65
Europe convenes 120-nation summit to assert space independence
French President Emmanuel Macron opened a two-day International Space Summit in Paris drawing officials, astronauts and industry leaders from approximately 120 countries, calling for innovation and investment to build a Europe that is independent even in space. The summit is the clearest institutional expression yet of European intent to reduce reliance on US launch and satellite services, a priority that sharpened after Starlinks centrality to Ukraine exposed both the value and the vulnerability of dependence on a single commercial provider.

NORMAL North America HIGH · 60
Roman Space Telescope launched on $4.3B Falcon Heavy mission
NASAs Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope, a $4.3 billion mission, launched successfully aboard a Falcon Heavy rocket and is now en route to its operating location at the L2 Lagrange point. Using a donated Hubble-class mirror originally built for a spy satellite, Roman carries a 300-megapixel wide-field camera capable of scanning the cosmos 1,000 times faster than Hubble and is expected to downlink 2,500 terabytes of data over its five-year primary mission.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
Romans successful launch validates Falcon Heavy as a heavy-lift vehicle for flagship science missions and opens a multi-year pipeline of data products relevant to defence-adjacent remote sensing technology development.

URGENT Middle East CRITICAL · 95
Iran cluster warheads hit Muwaffaq Salti, defeating Patriot screens
Iran has employed ballistic missiles carrying cluster warheads — dispersing multiple submunitions across a broad area rather than concentrating blast at a single point — against American forces at Muwaffaq Salti Air Base in Jordan, expanding a capability first demonstrated in the 12-Day War with Israel in June 2025 and used at greater scale since the conflict widened on 28 February 2026. Defense News confirmed the Jordan strike damaged one A-10 Thunderbolt II and caused light damage to roughly eight F-15s, with the US requiring approximately 30 Patriot interceptors to counter the attack amid growing concern about US air defence inventory levels.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
Cluster warhead ballistic missiles structurally complicate point-defence intercept geometry in a way that single unitary warheads do not, meaning existing Patriot battery configurations consume interceptors at a higher rate per incoming missile and accelerate the inventory depletion concern already flagged by the Center for Strategic and International Studies.
