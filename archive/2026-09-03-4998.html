Rocket Labs Neutron fairing clears all testing
Rocket Lab announced on August 27 that the Neutron medium-lift rockets Hungry Hippo fairing section completed all necessary testing before first flight, including opening and closing of the fairing halves in flight-like conditions. The Hungry Hippo fairings are permanently attached to Neutrons first stage and open during flight to deploy the second stage, eliminating the need for fairing recovery vessels or ocean-splashdown refurbishment.

Clearing this structural milestone with Neutron targeting a debut by year-end strengthens Rocket Labs position as a credible medium-lift competitor and supports the case for its US Space Force Space Systems Command contract, which is already signed.

SpaceX has announced plans for Starbase, Louisiana at Pecan Island in Vermilion Parish, describing it as the largest private spaceport in the world, featuring 10 Starship launch pads, a large production facility, and a first launch targeted for 2029. ESA commits €543.6m to three rocket startups
ESA signed the first contracts under its European Launcher Challenge, awarding €197.8 million to Isar Aerospace, €186.9 million to Rocket Factory Augsburg, and €158.9 million to PLD Space, with a fourth contract for MaiaSpace still being finalised. URGENT Global HIGH · 62
US, China arm hunter satellites for orbital warfare
A secretive Chinese spaceplane released a small satellite in June that, according to US space-tracking firm LeoLabs, looped around another Chinese satellite before returning to the spaceplane, in an orbital region crowded with commercial and military assets. Chinese researchers linked to the military are developing technologies including satellites designed to pursue or capture other spacecraft and systems to extend fuel supplies during such operations, while the US and Britain conducted their first joint military space operation in the path of a suspected Chinese spy satellite, according to commercial space-tracking data.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
The documented progression from observation to proximity operations by both sides raises the assessed probability of kinetic or non-kinetic space incidents during any future near-peer conflict, increasing insurance, resilience-architecture, and space-domain-awareness demand for operators and investors.

NORMAL North America HIGH · 60
Roman Space Telescope launches on Falcon Heavy
NASA and SpaceX successfully launched the Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope aboard a Falcon Heavy from Launch Complex 39A at Kennedy Space Center, with the observatory now travelling to the Sun-Earth Lagrange Point 2 on a mission valued at $4.3 billion. Roman carries a 300-megapixel wide-field camera and will scan the cosmos 1,000 times faster than Hubble, collecting data on dark energy, dark matter and potentially habitable exoplanets over a five-year primary mission.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
A successful Falcon Heavy delivery of a $4.3 billion observatory validates the vehicle as the preferred heavy-lift option for high-value government science payloads at a moment when Starship orbital reliability is still being established.

Army awards $192m to Palantir and Anduril for TITAN
The US Army awarded a combined $192 million to Palantir Technologies and Anduril Industries to move the Tactical Intelligence Targeting Access Node programme from prototyping to production, with Palantir receiving $127 million as prime contractor and Anduril receiving $65 million to manufacture ruggedised vehicle-mounted systems and integrate power and thermal subsystems.
