SpaceX targets Tuesday for Bandwagon-5 launch

UARX Space recovers OSSIE-D after comms blackout
Spanish startup UARX Space experienced a communications blackout immediately after launching its OSSIE-D orbital transfer vehicle on SpaceXs Transporter-18 mission on 1 October, caused by a configuration error on the interface of the ground station service it was using. NORMAL North America CRITICAL · 82
Pentagon signs APKWS framework for up to 25,000 kits a year
The Defense Department signed a framework agreement with BAE Systems to purchase up to 25,000 Advanced Precision Kill Weapon System guidance kits annually for up to seven years, with a pathway to scale output to 35,000 kits per year for US allies through Foreign Military Sales; no contract cost was announced and Congress must still approve and fund the buy. BAE, which manufactures the kits in Hudson, New Hampshire and Austin, Texas, confirmed it will increase production while the two sides negotiate a multiyear contract, noting the 25,000 figure matches previously cited full-rate production capacity.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
The framework signals sustained US government demand for low-cost drone-intercept munitions driven by operational experience in the Iran conflict, and BAEs ability to scale toward 35,000 kits for allied customers through FMS creates a durable revenue pathway contingent on congressional appropriation.

Australia launches Land 6504 air defence tender
Australias Department of Defence issued an industry solicitation on 13 October for Project Land 6504, a medium-range ground-based air defence system for the Australian Army, driven by the need to defend against ballistic missiles in their terminal phase and subsonic air-breathing threats. NORMAL United States HIGH · 70
Army Cyber Command refines human oversight of AI agents
Lieutenant General Christopher Eubank, head of US Army Cyber Command, described an iterative daily and weekly process in which human teams observe AI agents performing tasks and a governance board decides whether each agent should be allowed to work without human intervention. Every AI agent is currently assigned the same work role as a human counterpart and operates only under safeguarded human-in-the-loop conditions, with Eubank noting that in two days an AI agent could complete work that took an analytics support officer six months.
