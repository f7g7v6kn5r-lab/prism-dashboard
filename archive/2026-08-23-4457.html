Trump Signs 1000-Launch-Per-Year Space Policy
President Trump has signed a new national space transportation policy mandating more than 1,000 US launches and reentries annually by 2030, up from 178 completed in the prior year. The policy directs federal agencies to identify new launch sites on government land, expedite application processes, and structurally expand Americas commercial space capacity.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
A binding federal target of this scale repositions launch infrastructure as a national strategic asset and directly expands the addressable contract base for Rocket Lab, Firefly, and emerging spaceport operators competing for government-designated sites.

PAC-3 MSE 800pct Surge Hits Production Bottleneck
The Pentagon has sought a nearly 800% surge in PAC-3 MSE missile production — from 357 units in the current fiscal year to 3,203 in FY2027 — and in July 2026 awarded Lockheed Martin a seven-year contract worth up to $53.86 billion to triple production capacity by 2030. The PAC-3 bottleneck is the most immediate constraint on Western air defence reconstitution and signals that RTX and Lockheed Martin face both a revenue opportunity and a delivery risk that could accelerate competitor programmes including Israeli and South Korean interceptor alternatives.
