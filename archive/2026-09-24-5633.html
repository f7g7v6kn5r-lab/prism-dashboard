URGENT North America CRITICAL · 78
SpaceX halts Falcon 9 reservations beyond 2028
SpaceX is no longer accepting launch reservations on the Falcon 9 beyond 2028, with the company betting that Starship will be flying reliably enough to absorb the Falcon 9 mission profile by that date. In 2025 the Falcon 9 alone accounted for nearly half of all orbital launches from Earth, achieving orbit 165 times, meaning the planned phase-out creates an immediate gap for satellite operators planning missions in the 2028-2030 window.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
Satellite operators and government agencies without a confirmed Starship manifest or an alternative provider face a narrowing launch window that will force procurement decisions and potentially drive up prices on competing vehicles well before 2028.

URGENT Europe CRITICAL · 88
Germany seeks PAC-3 MSE production on home soil
German Defence Minister Boris Pistorius has lobbied US defence leaders to allow PAC-3 MSE interceptor production in Germany, arguing an additional production site would address a global shortfall in the vital air-defence weapon, following an agreement reached with US counterpart Pete Hegseth at the Pentagon last week. Germany already produces PAC-2 interceptors through MBDA, while PAC-3 MSE is manufactured by Lockheed Martin under a longstanding cooperation arrangement with Rheinmetall in Germany, meaning any new production line would require US government and Lockheed Martin agreement on scope and technology transfer.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
If Washington approves PAC-3 MSE coproduction in Germany, Lockheed Martin and Rheinmetall both gain structurally higher long-run revenue visibility on the Wests most in-demand air-defence interceptor, while NATO allies reduce a critical single-point supply risk.

URGENT Europe CRITICAL · 85
Germany tables record €140B 2027 defence budget
German Defence Minister Boris Pistorius told reporters after a Bundestag defence committee hearing that lawmakers showed much agreement on his record 2027 budget request of nearly €140 billion, combining €109.7 billion from the core ministry budget — a 32.7 percent increase over 2026 — with roughly €30 billion from the expiring Sondervermögen Bundeswehr special fund. The Bundestag final vote is expected on 27 November following a budget committee reconciliation session on 12 November, and Pistorius announced a first-ever dedicated funding programme for civilian universities and research institutes working on defence-relevant technologies including artificial intelligence and unmanned systems.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
A €140 billion German defence budget moving toward parliamentary approval is the largest structural demand signal in European defence procurement this decade, and primes with German supply chains or coproduction agreements are positioned for sustained multi-year order flow.

US Navy destroyers strained by Middle East demands
As of 14 September, two US Navy guided-missile destroyers were operating in the Red Sea and another 13 in the Arabian Sea, according to the US Naval Institute fleet tracker, as US forces conduct operations against Iran and the USS Stout departed Naval Station Norfolk to begin deployment. ◈ WATCH: FAA orbital license approval for Starship Flight 14
The FAA license modification decision for Starship Flight 14, targeted for launch as soon as 28 September, is the single most consequential near-term trigger for the orbital launch market and Starship-dependent commercial operators.
▲ BULL CASE  /  ▼ BEAR CASE ▾
▲ BULL CASE
A green light by 28 September confirms the FAA can process Starship orbital licenses at pace with SpaceX cadence, validating the Starship manifest for Exolaunch, Space Cargo and Starlink Florida migration and reducing schedule risk priced into Starship-dependent operators.
▼ BEAR CASE
A slip past 28 September extends the window of Falcon 9 indispensability precisely as SpaceX has stopped taking Falcon 9 reservations beyond 2028, tightening the near-term capacity crunch and pushing satellite operators toward competing launch providers at higher cost.
🛡 DEFENCE
◈ WATCH: Bundestag vote on €140 billion 2027 defence budget
The Bundestag budget committee reconciliation session scheduled for 12 November, preceded by committee scrutiny already under way, will determine whether Germanys record €140 billion ($160 billion) 2027 defence budget clears in full, with direct consequences for European prime contractors and PAC-3 MSE co-production negotiations with Lockheed Martin.
▲ BULL CASE  /  ▼ BEAR CASE ▾
▲ BULL CASE
Parliamentary approval without material cuts locks in the €109.7 billion core ministry budget and sustains Berlins lobbying position for PAC-3 MSE production in Germany, opening a new Lockheed Martin workshare avenue and lifting order visibility for Rheinmetall and MBDA.
▼ BEAR CASE
Significant amendments or delays push procurement decisions into 2027, stall the dedicated university and AI research funding programme Pistorius announced, and weaken Berlins negotiating hand with Washington on PAC-3 co-production terms.

◈ WATCH: US-Iran follow-on contact after UNGA shuttle talks
The first US-Iran shuttle talks in months, conducted through Qatari mediators on the sidelines of the UN General Assembly with no face-to-face contact and no position change confirmed by either side, set up the next 7 days as the window in which both governments must signal whether a structured negotiation is viable or whether the ceasefire collapse of July is the durable baseline.

A second round of mediated contact with even a narrow agreed agenda reduces the probability of near-term military escalation, eases pressure on Arabian Sea destroyer deployments, and pulls oil prices off their conflict premium, lowering operating costs for Gulf-based logistics and energy operators.
