Rocket Labs Neutron fairing completes all testing
Rocket Lab announced on 27 August that the Hungry Hippo fairing section of its Neutron medium-lift rocket has completed all required testing, including opening and closing of the fairing halves in flight-like conditions, ahead of the vehicles targeted debut by year-end. NORMAL North America HIGH · 70
NASA Roman telescope launches aboard Falcon Heavy
NASA and SpaceX successfully launched the Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope on a Falcon Heavy from Launch Complex 39A at Kennedy Space Center, sending the $4.3 billion observatory toward the Sun-Earth Lagrange Point 2 where it will conduct wide-field infrared surveys at 1,000 times the speed of Hubble. Both Falcon Heavy side boosters returned to land at Cape Canaveral, while the center core was expended to maximise performance for the payload.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
Romans successful launch validates the Falcon Heavy as a reliable workhorse for flagship science missions, reinforcing SpaceXs position in the high-value government launch market and signalling continued NASA investment in astrophysics infrastructure.

NORMAL Europe HIGH · 68
ESA commits €543.6M to three launcher startups
The European Space Agency signed the first contracts under its European Launcher Challenge, awarding €197.8 million to Isar Aerospace, €186.9 million to Rocket Factory Augsburg and €158.9 million to PLD Space, with a fourth contract involving MaiaSpace still being finalised. NORMAL North America HIGH · 62
SLS advances with four vehicles in concurrent production
NASA and contractor teams are stacking Solid Rocket Booster segments for Artemis III on Mobile Launcher 1 while installing RS-25 engines on Core Stage 3 for Artemis III, which is currently targeted for launch no earlier than June 2027 to fly Orion and a crew of four in Earth orbit to test lunar modules. ◆ PRISM ANALYSIS

NORMAL North America CRITICAL · 90
Army awards $465M for first laser production contract
The US Army awarded AeroVironment a $464.8 million production contract for the Enduring-High Energy Laser programme under an Other Transaction Agreement, the first production contract for a directed-energy system in US military history. The agreement covers delivery of dozens of 30 kW LOCUST laser weapons capable of defeating Group 1 through 3 unmanned aircraft systems, following disappointing field feedback on the Directed Energy Maneuver Short-Range Air Defense prototypes in the Middle East.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
The shift from prototype to production for a directed-energy counter-drone system validates the technology category as programme-of-record material and establishes a competitive benchmark that will pressure legacy interceptor suppliers as the Army seeks cost-effective alternatives to missile-based air defence in high-tempo operations.

Pentagon tests AI CUAS turret against drone swarms
The Pentagon and its agencies are testing the Edda portable, autonomous, AI-driven hard-kill counter-uncrewed aerial system turret, supplied by Spotter Global and 9 Mothers, against coordinated drone swarms, with the suppliers claiming engagement costs starting at $7 per target.
