SpaceX commits $100B to Starbase Louisiana
SpaceX will invest $100 billion in Starbase Louisiana, a new Starship launch complex spanning 125,000 acres with ten launch pads, with the first launch targeted for 2029. URGENT North America CRITICAL · 90
Boeing wins $131B F-15 Eagle Crest contract
The US Air Force has awarded Boeing a $131 billion ceiling indefinite-delivery, indefinite-quantity contract for the F-15 Eagle Crest programme through 2037, covering aircraft production, systems integration, modernisation, upgrades, retrofits and sustainment for both US and international variants. The award is one of the largest single defence contracts in history and locks Boeing into a programme of record position spanning the full F-15 lifecycle, with Foreign Military Sales scope explicitly naming Japan, Israel, Saudi Arabia, South Korea, Singapore, Indonesia and Poland.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
A $131 billion ceiling through 2037 provides Boeing a revenue floor that partially offsets continued commercial aerospace programme risk and signals that allied F-15 operators including Japan, Saudi Arabia, South Korea, Singapore, Indonesia and Poland face aligned lifecycle cost commitments for the next decade.
