Iran attacked two tankers affiliated with Abu Dhabi National Oil Company while they transited the Strait of Hormuz on the evening of Aug. 14, 2026, with the UAE Foreign Ministry condemning the strikes as acts of piracy and a flagrant violation of UN principles of freedom of navigation (Al Jazeera, Aug. 14, 2026). The brief's characterisation of a near-standstill in Hormuz traffic remains consistent with ongoing reporting; the broader coercive use of the strait as leverage by Iran is confirmed by multiple sources.

Disruption to Hormuz transit directly threatens Gulf energy supply chains and accelerates demand for maritime surveillance, escort systems, and drone-intercept technologies across the region.
