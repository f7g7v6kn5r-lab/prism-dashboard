Six months into the US-Israel war on Iran, the structural story is the militarization of geopolitical chokepoints — the Strait of Hormuz remains severely constrained at roughly ten transits per day against a pre-war baseline of approximately 85, oil is up 22 percent, and the US has already spent an estimated $37.5 billion on a conflict whose architects have not achieved their stated objectives, forcing a simultaneous reorientation of basing strategy, alliance management, and defence procurement toward a longer, costlier horizon. Watch the Falcon Heavy launch of NASAs Roman Space Telescope on 30 August — if it succeeds, it validates SpaceXs heavy-lift reliability at a moment when the Pentagon is leaning harder on commercial launch for national security payloads and the outcome will sharpen investor calculus on defence-space integration.

Falcon Heavy to launch Roman telescope Sunday
SpaceX is set to launch NASAs Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope aboard a Falcon Heavy rocket on 30 August, marking one of the most scientifically significant NASA missions in years.
