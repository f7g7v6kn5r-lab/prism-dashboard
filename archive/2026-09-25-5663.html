URGENT North America CRITICAL · 82
SpaceX gets exclusive DoD classified tracking data
The Defense Department has signed a contractual agreement giving SpaceX exclusive access to the most classified version of US Space Commands High Accuracy Catalog of on-orbit objects, according to a dozen government and industry sources cited by Breaking Defense. The arrangement, brokered through the Space Forces Joint Commercial Operations Cell, trades SpaceX data from its Stargaze star-tracking cameras for HAC access described as for internal use only, though it is unclear whether the data influences products SpaceX provides to non-military operators.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
Exclusive government data access of this kind creates a structural competitive moat for SpaceX in commercial space situational awareness services, directly threatening the market position of independent space-tracking firms and raising conflict-of-interest questions that regulators and rival operators will likely challenge.

Starship Flight 14 targets 28 Sep orbital debut
SpaceX has assembled its Starship upper stage S41 atop Super Heavy booster B21 at Starbase in Texas, with a first orbital flight targeted for no earlier than 28 September at 7:15 a.m. CDT, pending FAA approval. Starfish Space Otter set for Oct 1 orbital debut
Tukwila-based Starfish Space is preparing to launch its first full-size Otter spacecraft no earlier than 1 October aboard SpaceXs Transporter-18 rideshare mission from Vandenberg Space Force Base, carrying a 300-kilogram servicing vehicle roughly the size of a kitchen oven. URGENT North America HIGH · 72
Air Force cuts all civilian posts at China space institute
The US Air Force will eliminate all ten civilian positions at the China Aerospace Studies Institute on 1 October, with CASI Director Brendan Mulvaney stating the move will effectively end the institute even as Air Force officials claim the mission will continue through two master sergeant billets and an unfilled deputy post. All research at CASI has historically been conducted by the civilian personnel being eliminated, meaning the unclassified analytical output on PLA air, space and missile forces will cease unless the service reverses course.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
Eliminating CASI degradates the US open-source intelligence baseline on PLA space and missile capabilities at the precise moment China is accelerating military purges and Taiwan-adjacent force modernisation, raising the analytical risk premium for any programme or investment dependent on PLA threat assessments.

NORMAL North America HIGH · 65
NASA PRIMA telescope enters Phase B development
NASA has moved the Probe far-Infrared Mission for Astrophysics, PRIMA, into Phase B development, authorising further preliminary design work and technical and budget reviews, with a maximum project cost of $1.2 billion excluding launch costs if it advances to Phase C. The mission will be developed by NASAs Jet Propulsion Laboratory in collaboration with Goddard Space Flight Center, Marshall Space Flight Center and the space agencies of Canada, France, Germany, Japan, South Korea and the United Kingdom, targeting a 2033 launch.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
A seven-nation partnership at Phase B with a $1.2 billion ceiling creates procurement and subcontract opportunities for optical and infrared detector suppliers across North America, Europe and Asia-Pacific well before a Phase C commitment is made.

Moog expands thruster clean room 80% for Golden Dome
Moog Inc has completed and commissioned a new ISO Class 8 certified clean room at its Niagara Falls facility, increasing dedicated clean room capacity by more than 80 percent and enabling continuous multi-shift thruster production across assembly, integration and hot-fire testing. URGENT Europe CRITICAL · 90
NATO F-16s fail to intercept Russian drone over Romania
A Russian Geran strike drone entered Romanian airspace near Botoșani on Thursday morning during a major Russian attack on Ukraine involving more than 200 weapons including hypersonic and ballistic missiles, flew for approximately four minutes and crashed in a forest near Solca in Suceava County without being intercepted by two Romanian F-16s scrambled from Borcea airfield. The incident occurred in parallel with separate drone violations over Moldova, and Romanian Defence Minister Radu Miruță attributed the failure to intercept to dynamic circumstances and peacetime rules of engagement.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
Consecutive NATO airspace violations by low-cost Russian drones, combined with a reported CIA warning of Mediterranean drone attacks from commercial vessels, signal that the Alliances existing air-defence architecture cannot reliably counter this threat class, accelerating demand for low-altitude intercept systems and counter-drone sensors across Eastern and Southern Europe.

URGENT Europe CRITICAL · 82
CIA reportedly warned Europe of Russian Mediterranean drone plot
Spanish daily El Mundo reported, citing an anonymous Lithuanian intelligence source, that the CIA warned European countries of a suspected Russian plot to launch Gerbera-type drones from commercial vessels in the Mediterranean, with Spain, France or Italy identified as potential targets. The Gerbera drone has a wingspan of 2.5 metres, a maximum range of 600 kilometres and a warhead sized to start fires or destroy exposed infrastructure, and was described as transportable in shipping containers before catapult launch.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS

NORMAL Europe CRITICAL · 80
Italy signs 3.7 billion euro deal for two DDX destroyers
Italy has signed a 3.7 billion euro contract brokered by European procurement office OCCAR for two DDX destroyers to be built by Orizzonte Sistemi Navali, a joint venture of Fincantieri holding 51 percent and Leonardo holding 49 percent, with delivery scheduled for 2033 and 2035. The 13,500-tonne, 180-metre vessels will carry a crew of 251, feature Leonardos SADOC 5 command-and-control system, a digital S-band AESA radar, and MBDA weapon systems.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
The DDX contract consolidates a major revenue pipeline for Fincantieri and Leonardo through the mid-2030s and validates OCCAR as a vehicle for sovereign European naval procurement at scale, making both companies direct beneficiaries of continued European defence budget expansion.

NORMAL Europe CRITICAL · 78
Germany tables 140 billion euro defence budget for 2027
German Defence Minister Boris Pistorius told reporters after a Bundestag defence committee hearing that lawmakers showed much agreement on his ministrys record 2027 budget request of nearly 140 billion euros, combining 109.7 billion euros from the core ministry budget — a 32.7 percent increase over 2026 — with roughly 30 billion euros from the expiring Sondervermogen Bundeswehr special fund. The final Bundestag vote is expected on 27 November following a budget committee reconciliation session on 12 November, with Pistorius also announcing the ministrys first-ever dedicated funding programme for civilian universities working on defence-relevant AI and unmanned systems.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
A politically smooth path for Germanys €140 billion ($160 billion) defence budget would establish the largest single European defence procurement envelope in the post-Cold War era, creating multi-year demand signals for AI, unmanned systems and land-platform suppliers operating in the German market.

NORMAL United States HIGH · 60
Navy AI contract targets shipyard throughput bottleneck
In December 2025 the US Navy awarded a contract to accelerate adoption of artificial intelligence and autonomy technologies across the submarine industrial base, treating a shipyard throughput bottleneck as a data and software problem rather than a capacity or funding one. Huntington Ingalls finished its second quarter of 2026 with a total backlog of approximately $57.3 billion, illustrating the scale of the delivery constraint the AI programme is intended to address, with General Dynamics Electric Boat reporting that submarine schedule planning was reduced from 160 manual hours to under ten minutes during pilot deployments.

Framing shipyard delay as a data problem rather than a physical capacity problem opens a durable contract pipeline for AI and autonomy vendors targeting naval industrial clients, particularly as the backlog scale means the programme cannot be cancelled without worsening delivery timelines.
