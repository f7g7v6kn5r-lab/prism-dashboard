BREAKING Asia-Pacific CRITICAL · 88
LandSpace lands orbital booster in Chinese first
LandSpace became the first commercial Chinese company to successfully land an orbital-class rocket booster, achieving the milestone with its Zhuque-3 vehicle on its second flight on 19 August 2026. However, video and on-site footage confirmed that residual propellant ignited shortly after touchdown, producing a post-landing fire that burned the landing-gear area and caused the booster to collapse -- LandSpace has begun on-site inspections and says it aims to refurbish and fly the recovered stage within months. The landing nonetheless marks China's first successful land recovery of an orbital-class booster using landing legs. The second Chinese entity to achieve booster recovery is the state-backed CALT with its CZ-10B rocket, not the Long March-10B programme as previously stated.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
Chinese commercial reusability closing the gap on SpaceX redefines the competitive cost baseline for launch services globally and creates pricing pressure on Western providers including Rocket Lab and future Amazon LEO supply chains.
