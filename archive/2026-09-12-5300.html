NORMAL North America HIGH · 72
SpaceX launches classified USSF-153 payload to LEO
A Falcon 9 rocket lifted off from Space Launch Complex 4 East at Vandenberg Space Force Base on 10 September carrying the classified USSF-153 mission, an undisclosed number of satellites for the US Space Force to low Earth orbit. Booster B1081 completed its 27th flight and landed on the droneship Of Course I Still Love You, marking the 224th recovery on that vessel and the 660th Falcon booster landing to date, the fifth national security payload SpaceX has launched this year.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
SpaceX consolidating five national security launches in a single year cements its dominance of the US government manifest and raises the barrier for any competing launch provider seeking NSSL contract share.

Poland and ICEYE sign letter of intent for military satcom

Houthis seize Mayun island, threatening Red Sea oil route
Houthi forces captured Mayun island at the entrance of the Red Sea, confirmed by a senior Yemeni military official and a Houthi official, seizing a key alternative oil export chokepoint as Saudi Arabias cross-country East-West pipeline was shut down after drone attacks launched from Iraqi territory. BREAKING Middle East CRITICAL · 95
Iran strike damages A-10 and eight F-15s at Jordan base
Iranian strikes on Muwaffaq Salti Air Base in Jordan destroyed one A-10 Thunderbolt II, leaving it with a missing wing, and caused light damage to roughly eight F-15s that were subsequently returned to service, a US official said on condition of anonymity. The attack required US forces to fire approximately 30 Patriot interceptors, deepening concern over air defence interceptor inventory at a point when the Congressional Research Service had already recorded 42 US military aircraft lost or damaged and 18 service members killed since the conflict began in February.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
Expenditure of roughly 30 Patriot interceptors in a single engagement, combined with 42 aircraft already lost or damaged, signals that US munitions and platform reserves in the theatre are under severe attrition pressure that will accelerate procurement urgency for interceptors and aircraft sustainment in the near term.

URGENT Middle East CRITICAL · 90
Bahrain refuses Iran Hormuz meeting as Tehran seeks toll control
Bahrain stated it will not participate in Irans proposed Muscat consultation on Monday regarding Hormuz passage, saying security cannot be preserved through appeasement and demanding the strait remain open without discrimination, fees or permits. Iran has been in negotiations with Oman over formalising control of the waterway, including potentially charging passage fees, after the strait was effectively closed to Gulf oil exports following the US and Israeli strikes on Iran in late February that killed Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei.

Bahrains boycott of the Muscat talks hardens the Gulf states position against Iranian toll-charging and increases the probability that the Hormuz dispute escalates into a formal legal and economic confrontation that disrupts commodity and shipping markets beyond the current military context.

NORMAL North America CRITICAL · 78
Pentagon seeks AI system to track space and missile threats
The Defense Innovation Unit issued a solicitation due 24 September for the Space Threat Intelligence Synthesis Engine, an AI-based software tool to fuse multi-source sensor data including live video, satellite imagery, radar feeds and classified intelligence reports into continuously updated space and missile threat assessments fast enough to drive operational decisions. The system is intended to generate confidence-scored alerts supporting analyst-in-the-loop, human-on-the-loop and automated workflows, addressing current tool failures to distinguish closely spaced objects and track emerging threats.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
The solicitation represents a direct procurement signal for AI-enabled space domain awareness capabilities and positions defence-AI firms with multi-source data fusion experience at the front of a fast-moving Pentagon spending priority.

URGENT United States / China CRITICAL · 78
Anthropic report claims Claude used for Taiwan strike modelling
Anthropic published a threat intelligence report stating that a China-based actor linked to the Peoples Liberation Army Academy of Military Sciences used its Claude AI model to develop an electronic-warfare and air-defence suppression software suite that modelled radar jamming and ranked targets, including a simulation updated to cover 12 targets in Taiwan such as early-warning radars, Patriot and Tien Kung missile batteries, air bases and a command bunker. A separate actor allegedly used Claude to develop fire-control software for a Chinese navy anti-torpedo system; Anthropic said it banned the accounts involved. China's foreign ministry said it was not aware of the report.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
Documented misuse of a commercial large language model to simulate strike planning against specific allied air-defence assets demonstrates that AI guardrails are a live operational security gap, raising urgency for defence-sector AI procurement policies and export controls on frontier models.
