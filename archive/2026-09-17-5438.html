US lacks plans for nuclear attack in space, report finds
An April 2026 report by the American Foreign Policy Council, analysing two 2025 war games involving former officials from the National Security Council, the Pentagon, the intelligence community, the Space Force and US Space Command, found the US unprepared in every scenario tested for a nuclear detonation in orbit. URGENT Middle East CRITICAL · 95
Iran war cost $38B and depleted two-thirds of US interceptors
The Congressional Budget Office released an analysis finding the US military has incurred approximately $38 billion in costs from combat operations against Iran through 1 August, with the conflict consuming between one-half and two-thirds of the combined US inventory of Patriot, THAAD, Standard Missile-3 and Standard Missile-6 interceptors since June 2025. The CBO estimated the Pentagon would need $21.7 billion to replace expended munitions, of which $13.1 billion relates to interceptors alone, and warned rebuilding stocks could take at least five years even with increased production rates.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
The confirmed strategic interceptor shortfall is the most consequential single supply-chain fact in the current defence environment, as it simultaneously constrains US deterrence credibility in the Pacific, limits allied support to Ukraine and Saudi Arabia, and drives a multi-year compulsory ramp in interceptor production that benefits RTX and LMT across their missile defence portfolios.
