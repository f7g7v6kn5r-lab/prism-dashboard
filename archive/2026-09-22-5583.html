Trump called off Houthi strikes after bombs were loaded, report says
The New York Times, citing US administration officials, reported that Trump instructed the Pentagon to prepare airstrikes against Houthi targets after speaking with Saudi Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman on Thursday, reversing an earlier position against strikes, but called off the operation by Sunday when commanders had approved target lists and personnel were loading bombs onto aircraft. URGENT United States CRITICAL · 82
Pentagon munitions surge data stays private after deadline
Deputy Defense Secretary Steve Feinberg gave industry until 26 August to submit plans for faster and higher-volume production of 16 critical weapons programmes, but 26 days after that deadline the Defense Department has not disclosed how many companies responded or what timelines they offered. The request came as the Iran war drew down US Patriot interceptor stocks from roughly 2,330 before the war to between 759 and 827 by end of July, with CSIS estimating 42-month production timelines for PAC-3 MSE and 53 months for THAAD interceptors.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
The absence of public disclosure on industry responses conceals the scale and pace of the coming munitions investment cycle, leaving defence investors without visibility on which primes and suppliers are positioned for accelerated production contracts ahead of the fiscal year 2028 budget request.
