SpaceX plans $100B Starbase Louisiana with 10 pads
SpaceX has announced a $100 billion investment in Starbase Louisiana, a dedicated Starship launch complex spanning 125,000 acres with ten launch pads, with the first launch targeted for 2029. The facility would become the largest private spaceport in the world by land area and pad count, anchoring the next phase of Starship operational cadence beyond the Texas site.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
A $100 billion private spaceport commitment at this scale reshapes the competitive landscape for launch infrastructure investment and signals that SpaceX is engineering for a flight rate that existing Gulf Coast facilities cannot absorb, creating downstream demand for range services, workforce, and logistics providers across the region.

NASA Roman telescope nears Falcon Heavy launch
NASAs Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope has completed final integration and launch rehearsal milestones and is scheduled to lift off on a Falcon Heavy rocket, according to NASASpaceFlight. Boeing wins $131B F-15 sustainment deal to 2037
The US Air Force has awarded Boeing a contract worth up to $131 billion to sustain F-15 Eagle and F-15EX aircraft through 2037, covering over a decade of maintenance, upgrades, and support for both domestic and international fleets, according to Shephard Media and Breaking Defense.
