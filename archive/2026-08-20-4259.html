China lands orbital booster — Zhuque-3 historic first
Chinas Landspace became the first private Chinese company to recover an orbital-class booster on land, with the second flight of the Zhuque-3 Y2 rocket on Aug 18-19, 2026 (liftoff 2335 UTC Aug 18; touchdown Aug 19 local time). The first stage landed vertically at a designated site in Minqin County, Gansu Province, approximately 390 km downrange. State-owned CASC had previously recovered a booster at sea via the Long March 10B in July 2026, making Landspace the second Chinese entity overall to recover an orbital booster and the first to do so on land. Multiple credible sources including SpaceNews, Space.com and Xinhua confirm the milestone, which narrows the gap between Chinese commercial launch and SpaceX Falcon 9 reusability capability.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
A reusable Chinese commercial orbital booster now exists outside state CNSA programs — this directly threatens Western launch pricing power in Asia-Pacific markets and accelerates the timeline for Chinese constellation deployment at reduced cost.
