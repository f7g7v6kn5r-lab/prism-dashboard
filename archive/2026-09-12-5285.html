URGENT North America CRITICAL · 75
Pentagon seeks AI engine for space and missile threat fusion
The Defense Innovation Unit issued a solicitation due 24 September for a Space Threat Intelligence Synthesis Engine, an AI-based software tool designed to fuse live video, satellite imagery, radar feeds, geospatial data and classified intelligence reports into continuously updated, confidence-scored alerts for missile and space threat identification. The solicitation states that existing tools struggle to distinguish closely spaced objects and track emerging threats at the speed required for operational decisions.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
The DIU solicitation is a direct procurement signal for AI and sensor-fusion firms, opening a near-term contract pathway that is directly tied to wartime operational gaps the Iran conflict has exposed in US space and missile defence situational awareness.

SpaceX launches classified USSF-153 payload to LEO
SpaceX launched the USSF-153 mission on a Falcon 9 from Space Launch Complex 4 East at Vandenberg Space Force Base, delivering an undisclosed number of satellites to low Earth orbit for the US Space Force. Booster B1081 completed its 27th flight and landed on the droneship Of Course I Still Love You, marking the 224th landing on that vessel and the 660th Falcon booster landing to date.

Poland signs LoI with ICEYE for military satellite comms
Finnish space company ICEYE and Polands state-run defence group PGZ signed a letter of intent at the MSPO defence show in Kielce to jointly develop a military satellite communications system, with Polish Prime Minister Donald Tusk describing the goal as a Polish Starlink to be implemented from 2026 to 2030. NORMAL North America HIGH · 62
Roman Space Telescope launched on $4.3B Falcon Heavy mission
NASAs Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope launched successfully on a Falcon Heavy, beginning a $4.3 billion mission to conduct wide-field imaging from the L2 point; its 300-megapixel wide-field camera will cover the equivalent of 45 city blocks in a single image and will downlink 2,500 terabytes of data over its five-year primary mission, compared with 172 terabytes returned by Hubble in 30 years. The telescope uses a Hubble-class mirror originally built for a spy satellite and will target dark matter, dark energy and potentially habitable exoplanets.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
Romans successful launch validates the Falcon Heavy as the vehicle of choice for large flagship observatory missions and opens a new data-volume era for astronomy and Earth-science commercial derivative applications, with 2,500 terabytes of downlink data creating downstream opportunities for ground-segment processing and analytics providers.

NORMAL Asia-Pacific HIGH · 57
LandSpace lands orbital booster in Chinese commercial first
Chinese commercial launch company LandSpace landed the first stage of its ZhuQue-3 rocket in Minqin County, Gansu Province on 19 August, becoming the first commercial Chinese company to recover an orbital-class booster on land; a post-landing fire broke out in the aft section but the mission was declared successful as a reusability demonstration. URGENT Middle East CRITICAL · 95
Iran damaged NSA Bahrain and Jordan base, US admits
Acting US Navy Secretary Hung Cao confirmed in an interview that Iranian forces blew the hell out of the Fifth Fleet headquarters at Naval Support Activity Bahrain, while Iranian strikes on Tuesday damaged multiple US aircraft at Muwaffaq Salti Air Base in Jordan, including one A-10 Thunderbolt II left with a missing wing and approximately eight F-15s that sustained light damage and were returned to service. A Congressional Research Service report cited by Defense News stated that 42 US military aircraft had already been lost or damaged in the conflict before the Jordan strike, and 18 service members have been killed with over 800 injured since the war began in February.

Official US acknowledgement of severe infrastructure and aircraft attrition at two major Middle East bases forces investors and operators to price in the possibility that the current forward posture is not sustainable without significant recapitalisation of interceptor stocks, aircraft and base infrastructure -- a multi-billion-dollar demand signal for primes and munitions suppliers.

URGENT Asia-Pacific CRITICAL · 90
Anthropic claims Claude used to model Taiwan air defence targets
Anthropic said in a threat intelligence report published Thursday that a China-based actor used its Claude AI models to develop electronic-warfare and air-defence suppression software that modelled radar jamming and ranked 12 targets in Taiwan, including Patriot and Tien Kung missile batteries, early-warning radar, air bases and a command bunker; Anthropic said account information linked the actor to the Peoples Liberation Army Academy of Military Sciences. A separate China-based actor allegedly used Claude to develop specifications and fire-control software for a Chinese navy anti-torpedo system; Anthropic banned the relevant accounts and Chinas foreign ministry said it was not aware of the report.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
The confirmed use of a commercial AI model to plan suppression of US-allied air defences in Taiwan validates adversarial AI misuse as a live threat vector and accelerates the case for operators and governments to invest in AI access controls, red-team auditing and sovereign model infrastructure that cannot be co-opted through commercial API access.

Iran claims Saildrone seizure; US says all vessels accounted for
Iran shared a video purportedly showing a US Saildrone unmanned surface vessel near the Strait of Hormuz in flames, but a US official told Military Times that all Saildrones are accounted for and fully operational. The incident follows the confirmed capture on 8 September of an Anduril Dive-LD autonomous underwater vehicle, which US Navy Captain Tim Hawkins of US Central Command described as an older model that neither collected sensitive data nor carried classified sonar or radar equipment.

URGENT United States CRITICAL · 80
Pentagon pulls Anthropic from 90% of classified AI work
The Pentagon chief technology officer disclosed that approximately 90 percent of the Defense Departments classified AI workloads previously running on Anthropic technology have been migrated to alternative model providers, and all Anthropic technology was removed from the Maven Smart System months ago. The transition came despite a federal judge ruling in late August that the Departments earlier designation of Anthropic as a supply chain risk was illegal and baseless.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
The near-total removal of Anthropic from classified defence AI, irrespective of a court ruling against the designation, signals that procurement decisions in sensitive national security AI are being driven by geopolitical and trust considerations that legal challenges cannot quickly reverse.

Galaxia acquires hyperspectral imager for defence EO missions
Canadian space technology company Galaxia acquired a HyperScape100 hyperspectral imaging payload from European optical provider Simera Sense, enabling 32 user-selectable spectral bands from a library of 400 for commercial, civil and defence Earth observation missions.
