NORMAL Europe HIGH · 65
RAND warns hasty US Europe exit would hurt American arms sales
A RAND study released Wednesday, drawing on interviews with 51 officials and experts in France, Germany, Poland, and the UK conducted mostly between April and November 2025, found that a rapid, uncoordinated US military drawdown from Europe would push key NATO allies away from American weapons over the long term, damaging the US defence industrial base. The four allies studied see a stark difference between a coordinated withdrawal they feel they could handle and an abrupt one they say would create major capability gaps and potentially embolden Russia to attack a NATO member.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
The RAND finding that process and sequencing determine whether allied procurement stays aligned with US industry gives the ongoing Pentagon Europe review direct commercial stakes for the US defence industrial base that extend well beyond force-posture politics.
