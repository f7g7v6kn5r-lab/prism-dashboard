CENTCOM launches multinational attack drone task force
US Central Command announced the establishment of Task Force Falcon Strike on August 13, not August 17, as the first multidomain, multinational attack drone task force in CENTCOM history, employing one-way attack drones across air, surface, and sub-sea domains. As of August 19, CENTCOM has initiated consultations with and formally invited regional partners to join but has not yet identified or confirmed any named allied contributors; the task force will scale as partners formally join. Trump Threatens Oman as Hormuz Tensions Surge
US President Donald Trump on August 17 threatened to bomb Oman if it interfered with US efforts to reopen the Strait of Hormuz, after Iranian officials said Tehran had reached a shipping-management agreement with Oman regarding the waterway. The threat came as the 60-day US-Iran memorandum of understanding expired on August 17 without a broader peace deal; the MOU had largely broken down within weeks and Trump declared the ceasefire over in mid-July. Trump stated informal contacts are ongoing with Irans Revolutionary Guard Corps, which the IRGC denies, and said he is not in a hurry to make a deal. Shipping traffic through the strait remained severely constrained as of August 17, with reports of as few as 13 vessels transiting over the prior weekend.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
Direct US threats against Oman and continued Hormuz instability create acute risk for energy logistics and satellite-tracked maritime security platforms operating across one of the worlds most critical chokepoints.
