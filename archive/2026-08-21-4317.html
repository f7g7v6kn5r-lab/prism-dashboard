BREAKING East Asia CRITICAL · 88
Zhuque-3 lands booster — China joins reuse club
LandSpaces Zhuque-3 Y2 rocket completed its second flight on Aug 18 2026 and recovered its first stage on land at Minqin county, Gansu province, making LandSpace the first commercial Chinese company to land an orbital-class booster. However, video and on-site footage confirmed a post-touchdown fire ignited residual propellant, burning the landing-gear area and causing the stainless-steel booster to collapse, per SpaceNews and Particle News (Aug 19 2026). The structural milestone stands -- LandSpace also joins CASC, not SpaceX alone, as the achievement follows Chinas earlier sea-platform net-capture recovery of a Long March-10B booster in July 2026 -- but the recovered stage is damaged and no refly timeline has been confirmed.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
China's entry into reusable orbital-class booster operations compresses the timeline for competitive Chinese commercial launch pricing, directly threatening Western launch providers competing for international satellite deployment contracts.
