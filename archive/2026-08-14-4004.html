BREAKING Middle East CRITICAL · 90
CENTCOM Launches Multinational Attack Drone Task Force
US Central Command announced on Aug. 13, 2026 the establishment of Task Force Falcon Strike, described as the first-ever multi-domain multinational attack drone task force, integrating air, surface, and subsurface one-way attack drone systems. As of Aug. 14, CENTCOM has initiated the process of consulting with and formally inviting regional partners; no allied nations had been publicly confirmed as members. The unit is led by US Special Operations Command Central. CENTCOM declined to name which nations it expects to participate or which systems will be used. The task force builds on Task Force Scorpion Strike, established nine months prior as the first dedicated one-way attack drone squadron in the Middle East.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
Falcon Strike institutionalizes multinational drone warfare doctrine in the most operationally active US theatre, generating sustained procurement demand for one-way attack drones, multi-domain UAS integration platforms, and allied interoperability systems from companies including Anduril and DroneShield.

General Dynamics Paid $533M for Plant That Made Zero Shells
An investigation published across multiple outlets including Asia Times and Truthout reveals that General Dynamics received 533 million dollars in taxpayer funding to build and operate an advanced artillery shell manufacturing facility near Dallas that has produced no shells, with robotic systems catching fire repeatedly during the 2024 ramp-up period. General Dynamics has not been held publicly accountable and has not repaid any funds, according to the reporting.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
This failure, surfacing concurrent with the Pentagon's 21-day production mandate, intensifies congressional and investor scrutiny of cost-plus artillery and munitions contracts and raises the probability of mandatory performance clawback provisions being inserted into future large-volume munitions awards.
