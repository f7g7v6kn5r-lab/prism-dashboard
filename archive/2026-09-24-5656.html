URGENT North America CRITICAL · 82
SpaceX stacks Starship for orbital flight attempt
SpaceX assembled the full Starship-Super Heavy stack at Starbase in south Texas on Wednesday, with Ship 41 placed atop Booster 21 to reach a combined height of 124 meters. Liftoff of Flight 14, targeting a nearly 10-hour mission deploying 26 Version 3 Starlink satellites, is targeted for September 28 at 7:15 a.m. CDT pending FAA license approval, which has not yet been granted.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
FAA approval of the first orbital Starship license would validate the regulatory pathway for the national security and commercial payloads that multiple government and commercial operators have already contracted to fly on Starship, making the outcome a material watch item for defence-adjacent launch investors.

URGENT North America CRITICAL · 78
SpaceX gets exclusive DoD classified tracking access
The Defense Department has signed a contractual agreement giving SpaceX exclusive access to US Space Commands most classified High Accuracy Catalog of on-orbit objects through the Space Forces Joint Commercial Operations Cell, according to a dozen government and industry sources speaking to Breaking Defense. SpaceX provides its Stargaze star-tracking camera data in exchange, but commercial space monitoring firms are concerned the arrangement may allow the data to influence SpaceX products offered to non-military operators, potentially distorting the market.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
Exclusive access to the highest-fidelity government space tracking data gives SpaceX a structural competitive advantage over commercial space situational awareness firms, raising procurement fairness questions that could drive regulatory scrutiny and reshape the commercial SSA market.

URGENT North America HIGH · 72
Air Force cuts all civilian posts at China institute
The Air Force will eliminate all 10 civilian positions at the China Aerospace Studies Institute on October 1, which CASI Director Brendan Mulvaney says will effectively end the institute even as the Air Force argues its mission will continue through assigned military service members. Mulvaney stated that all research has always been performed by the civilian personnel being cut, directly contradicting the Air Force position that capabilities will be preserved.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
Gutting the only unclassified PLA air, space and missile research centre at the moment China is expelling senior Central Military Commission members removes a key open-source intelligence layer precisely when analytical demand is highest, a risk that defence-oriented research and intelligence firms could move to fill commercially.

CIA warned Europe of Russian drone plot via vessels
The CIA warned European countries of a suspected Russian plan to launch Gerbera-type drones from commercial vessels in the Mediterranean, targeting Spain, France or Italy, according to a report by Spanish daily El Mundo citing an anonymous Lithuanian intelligence source. Satrec Initiative to build optical satellites for Hanwha
South Korean company Satrec Initiative is developing four satellites capable of 10-centimeter-class optical imagery to complement parent company Hanwha Aerospaces 64-satellite SAR constellation, which is designed to achieve 30-minute global revisit by 2031, according to SpaceNews.
