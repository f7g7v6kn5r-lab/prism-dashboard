LandSpace Zhuque-3 Topples After Historic Booster Landing
LandSpace became the first commercial Chinese company to successfully land an orbital-class booster on legs during the second flight of its Zhuque-3 rocket, with the vehicle completing a powered descent and vertical touchdown, according to NASASpaceFlight and SpaceFlightNow. A post-touchdown fire ignited residual propellant, burning the landing-gear area and causing the stainless-steel booster to collapse — though LandSpace has begun on-site inspections and says it aims to refurbish and reflighthe recovered stage within months.
