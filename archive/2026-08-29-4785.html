Boeing wins $131B F-15 Eagle Crest contract
The US Air Force has awarded Boeing a $131.23 billion ceiling indefinite-delivery, indefinite-quantity contract for the F-15 Eagle Crest programme, covering aircraft production, systems integration, modernization, upgrades, retrofits and sustainment through August 2037, according to Defense News and the Department of War. The contract is one of the largest single-platform awards in Air Force history and also includes foreign military sales to seven allied nations, locking Boeing into a multi-decade airframe role at a moment when the company faces ongoing programme pressures elsewhere.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
The $131 billion ceiling provides Boeing with a long runway of revenue visibility in military aviation that partially offsets commercial and other programme risks, but the magnitude of the combined production and sustainment commitment also intensifies debate over whether resources are being diverted from next-generation capabilities and undersea readiness.

Pentagon blacklist of Anthropic struck down as illegal
