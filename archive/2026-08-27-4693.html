URGENT North America CRITICAL · 90
Boeing wins $131B F-15 Eagle Crest production and sustainment contract
The US Air Force has awarded Boeing a $131.23 billion ceiling indefinite-delivery/indefinite-quantity contract under the F-15 Eagle Crest programme, covering aircraft production, systems integration, modernization, upgrades, retrofits, and sustainment, with work expected to be completed by August 2037 and an ordering period running through 2031 with an option to extend through 2036. The contract, which also includes foreign military sales to Japan, Israel, Saudi Arabia, South Korea, Singapore, Indonesia and Poland, is one of the largest single awards in US Air Force history and secures Boeings defence revenue base at a time when its commercial aviation division continues to face delivery pressures.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
A $131 billion production and sustainment ceiling through 2037 insulates Boeings defence unit from near-term programme risk and signals that the Air Force is committed to the F-15 as a long-duration platform rather than accelerating a full transition to sixth-generation fighters, which will shape competitor investment theses around the F-47 timeline.
