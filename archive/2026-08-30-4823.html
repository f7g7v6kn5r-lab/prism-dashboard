Roman Space Telescope launches on $4.3B mission
NASA and SpaceX successfully launched the Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope aboard a Falcon Heavy rocket from Florida on Sunday, placing the $4.3 billion observatory on course for its L2 halo orbit. The telescope carries a 300-megapixel wide-field camera built around a Hubble-class mirror originally manufactured for a reconnaissance satellite and is expected to discover tens of thousands of exoplanets while probing dark matter and dark energy.
