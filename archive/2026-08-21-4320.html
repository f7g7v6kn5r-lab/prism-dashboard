BREAKING Asia-Pacific CRITICAL · 92
China lands orbital booster — first commercial recovery
LandSpace became the first commercial Chinese company to recover an orbital-class booster on land, with the Zhuque-3 Y2 rocket lifting off on 19 Aug 2026 (UTC+8) and successfully landing its first stage vertically at Minqin County, Gansu Province, approximately 390 km downrange. The mission also successfully delivered the Honghu-03 satellite to orbit. Post-touchdown video shows a fire ignited from residual propellant that burned the landing-gear area and caused the stainless-steel booster to collapse after landing; LandSpace has begun on-site inspections and states it aims to refurbish the stage for future reuse. The milestone follows CASCs sea-based net recovery of the Long March-10B booster in July 2026, and makes LandSpace the fourth entity globally -- after SpaceX, Blue Origin, and CASC -- to recover an orbital-class booster. The brief description of a clean recovery should be updated to note the post-touchdown fire and booster collapse, per Particle News reporting confirmed 19 Aug 2026.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
A commercially reusable Chinese orbital booster fundamentally alters the competitive cost baseline for launch services globally, threatening the pricing power of Western providers and accelerating Beijings ability to deliver LEO constellations at state-subsidised rates.
