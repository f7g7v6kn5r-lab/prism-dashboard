BREAKING Strait of Hormuz CRITICAL · 90
Hormuz Stays Shut as Iran Rejects US Diplomacy
The Strait of Hormuz remains effectively closed to commercial shipping as of Aug 19 2026, with one vessel transiting on Aug 16 against a normal baseline of approximately 73 per day, per live tracker data. Iranian parliament speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf reiterated on Aug 18 that the strait will remain closed until the US fulfils obligations under the defunct June 2026 Islamabad MOU -- conditions including removal of the naval blockade, unfreezing of Iranian assets, easing of sanctions and a halt to military operations, per the Washington Times. The brief reopening under the June 17 MOU broke down in early July after Iranian attacks on commercial vessels, and the MOU is now defunct per multiple sources. The claim that this ends a two-month diplomatic window that failed to produce a comprehensive settlement is broadly consistent with sourcing; the MOU was signed in June and collapsed in July.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
A prolonged Hormuz closure disrupts global energy logistics and sustains elevated threat environments that drive emergency defence procurement across the Indo-Pacific and European theatre.
