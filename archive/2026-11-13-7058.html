Intuitive Machines posts record $269M quarter
Intuitive Machines reported third-quarter 2026 revenue of $269.35 million, beating the $247.56 million consensus estimate and rising 413.7 percent year on year, with satellite production from the January Lanteris acquisition contributing $215.12 million of that growth. NORMAL North America HIGH · 60
AST SpaceMobile unveils Blacklift launch vehicle
AST SpaceMobile held an unveiling event in New York on 12 November to present its Blacklift launch vehicle, a three-stage rocket with a payload capacity of 20 tons to low Earth orbit, designed to carry up to five BlueBird satellites per launch with a target cadence of two launches per month. No hardware was shown beyond engine videos from Firefly Aerospace, whose engines AST is acquiring for the vehicle, and the company said it expects a first flight in late 2028.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
Widespread industry skepticism about a two-year development timeline for a new reusable vehicle, combined with the absence of physical hardware at the unveiling, means Blacklift is a schedule risk rather than a confirmed supply solution, and operators relying on AST for direct-to-device coverage should model continued dependence on the constrained commercial launch market.

NORMAL Europe HIGH · 50
Spain deploys first fully domestic orbital transfer vehicle
UARX Space launched OSSIE-D, an orbital transfer vehicle designed and built entirely in Spain with 75 percent of its components developed in-house, aboard a SpaceX Falcon 9, deploying it using the companys own proprietary separation system in what UARX Space described as a world first combining an orbital transfer vehicle and a separation system developed by the same company in a single mission. DHV Technology supplied the complete electrical power subsystem including solar arrays, power conditioning and distribution unit, main battery modules, solar array drive assembly and additional supporting subsystems.
◆ PRISM ANALYSIS
A fully domestic Spanish OTV marks a structural step in European in-space transportation sovereignty and opens a credible local supplier option for constellation operators and defence customers seeking alternatives to US or established European prime contractors.

NORMAL Europe / Baltic Sea region HIGH · 50
Balnord calls Europe to scale deep-tech for defence
Baltic Sea-focused venture capital firm Balnord published the third edition of its Dual-Use Founders Handbook, arguing that Europes main defence readiness challenge is no longer technology creation but deploying proven technologies at scale through reformed procurement, certification and industrial base development. The report, produced with contributions from the EIB Group, Invest Europe and the NATO Innovation Fund, identifies artificial intelligence, autonomous systems, commercial space, telecommunications, robotics and cybersecurity as the dual-use fields where civilian-first innovation must transition to hundreds or thousands of operational defence units rather than small test batches.
